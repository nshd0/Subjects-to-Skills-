## Part 1: Grade 6 — Track A (Compulsory Embedded)

**Scenario:** You are a Grade 6 teacher (Mathematics / Science / CT). Your school follows Track A (CT & AI embedded across core subjects per NCF-SE Middle Stage guidelines).

### Step-by-Step Test Procedure:
1. **Navigate to Grade 6 Hub:**
   - From the top navigation or Explore by Grade, navigate to `/grade/grade-6`.
2. **Examine the CBSE AI & Computational Thinking Curriculum section:**
   - Locate the Middle Stage track banner detailing **Track A (100 hrs/yr)**.
   - Verify the strand breakdown:
     - Advanced CT Skills: 40 hrs
     - Introductory AI Concepts: 20 hrs
     - Interdisciplinary Projects: 40 hrs
3. **Open Skill Node Detail Drawer (Skill Map or Grade Hub):**
   - Click on `Advanced CT: Nested Conditionals & Logical Flowcharts` or `Introductory AI: AI Fundamentals & Machine Perception`.
   - **Verification Checkpoints:**
     - [x] **Visual Badge:** Displays blue badge labeled `Compulsory Embedded • 40 hrs CT Strand` / `Compulsory Embedded • 20 hrs AI Strand`.
     - [x] **Track Profile Panel:**
       - Track Type: `Track A: Compulsory curriculum embedded into core subjects (Mathematics, EVS, Science, Social Science)`
       - Annual Hours: Displays specific strand duration (e.g. 40 hrs / 20 hrs out of 100 hrs/yr total).
       - Transition Pathway: Explains progression into Grade 7 functional abstraction & Grade 9 readiness.
       - Official Citation: Cites *CBSE CT&AI Curriculum, Classes 3-8, 2026-27: Middle Stage (Class 6)*.
4. **Launch Lesson Plan Wizard (`/planner/new`):**
   - Select **Grade 6**.
   - Open the **Subject** dropdown.
   - **Verification Checkpoints:**
     - [x] Core subjects listed along with `Computational Thinking & AI (Track A • Compulsory Embedded)` and `AI Skill Module (901) (Track B • Optional Skill Module)`.
     - [x] Select `Computational Thinking & AI`:
       - Associated units and skills populate automatically.
       - Focus skills include *Advanced CT: Nested Conditionals & Logical Flowcharts* and *Introductory AI: Machine Perception*.
5. **Create Lesson Plan:**
   - Enter a title (e.g. *"School Governance Flowcharting"*), select 1–2 focus skills, choose Bloom's taxonomy level (*Apply / Analyse*), and save draft.
   - [x] Wizard advances smoothly through all steps without hesitation or blocking validation errors.
6. **Launch Assessment Wizard (`/assess/new`):**
   - Select **Grade 6** → **Computational Thinking & AI**.
   - [x] Rubric matrix aligns to CBSE Middle Stage CT/AI learning outcomes across 4 performance descriptors: *Exemplary*, *Proficient*, *Developing*, *Beginning*.

## Part 4: Edge Cases & Robustness

1. **Rapid Grade Switching:**
   - Sequence: Navigate rapidly between Grade 3 (Foundational CT) → Grade 8 (Middle Track A/B) → Grade 9 (Secondary Track C) → Grade 11 (Senior Secondary).
   - **Result:** State re-evaluates cleanly. No stale track badges, no mismatched subject options.
2. **Same Skill / Multi-Disciplinary Context:**
   - Open Middle Stage CT&AI nodes accessed from Mathematics vs. Science vs. Social Science.
   - **Result:** The Track Profile panel maintains accurate metadata and appropriate interdisciplinary notes.
3. **Graceful Handling of Grades Without Specific Tracks:**
   - Select a Grade with no Track B/C offerings (e.g. Grade 3 only offers Track A Computational Thinking).
   - **Result:** Dropdown shows only valid subjects; no empty error states or broken layouts.
