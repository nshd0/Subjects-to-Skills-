# Grade 6 Artificial Intelligence: Data Basics & Algorithmic Foundations
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Artificial Intelligence / Skill Education (Middle Stage, Grade 6 - Foundational Bridge)  
**Curriculum Documents:** CBSE Class 6 AI Skill Curriculum (Unit 1: Introduction to AI & Data Literacy; Unit 2: Data Around Us); NCF-SE 2023 Middle Stage Vocational & Computational Thinking Goals C-7.1, C-7.2.  
**Focus:** Concrete-to-abstract transition: Demystifying AI through physical data collection, manual feature sorting, decision tree card games, and uncovering bias in data sets.

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner understands that Artificial Intelligence relies on structured data, systematically collects, cleans, and categorizes multi-modal data from classroom environments, constructs manual decision trees to simulate machine classification, and identifies examples of data bias or missing perspectives in everyday technology."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **CBSE AI Skill Curriculum Grade 6:** Module 1 (*What is AI vs Smart Machines*), Module 2 (*Data as Fuel for AI*), Module 3 (*Data Collection, Categorization, and Ethics*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Middle Stage Computational Thinking & Skill Education C-7.1 (Pattern recognition & data representation), C-7.2 (Ethical discernment in digital tools).
- **UNESCO AI Competency Framework for School Students:** Aspect 1 (Human-centred mindset), Aspect 2 (AI techniques and data).

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: Distinguishing AI from Rule-Based Devices:** Differentiate between automated pre-programmed appliances (washing machine, simple calculator) and learning systems (face unlock, recommendation engines).
- **C2: Data Collection & Feature Tagging:** Gather physical data points from classmates or nature; structure data into rows and columns with explicit feature attributes (labels).
- **C3: Manual Classification & Decision Trees:** Build paper-based binary classification trees (Yes/No branching) to categorize objects, animals, or leaves based on observable features.
- **C4: Identifying Dataset Bias & Inclusivity:** Investigate how incomplete or skewed datasets produce biased AI outputs (e.g., voice assistants failing on regional Indian accents).

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: Distinguishing AI from Rule-Based Devices

#### Task ID: `AI-6-DATA-1.1`
- **Title:** Classify everyday household gadgets into "Rule-Based Automated" vs "AI-Powered Learning" systems.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet or printed cards)
- **Skill Type:** Conceptual Distinction & Critical Thinking
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Peer Discussion
- **Track Alignment:** Track B (Optional Skill Module) / Track A (Embedded)
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Evaluates 8 gadgets (e.g., microwave oven, YouTube recommendation, ceiling fan regulator, smartphone voice assistant, barcode scanner, autonomous robot vacuum, digital alarm, optical character reader).
  - Justifies each classification using the test: "Does it improve with new data or merely execute fixed IF-THEN rules?"
  - Correctly categorizes at least 7 out of 8 items.
- **Source Citation:** CBSE AI Class 6 Facilitator Handbook, Unit 1, pp. 8–12.
- **Scaffold Support:** Two-column decision test card: "Rule-Based: Follows fixed formula forever | AI: Learns patterns from examples".
- **Extension Challenge:** "Can a rule-based washing machine become an AI machine? What sensors and data would it need to learn fabric stain types?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: कृत्रिम बुद्धिमत्ता (Kritrim Buddhimatta - AI), स्वचालित प्रणाली (Swachalit Pranalee - Automated System)
  - Tamil: செயற்கை நுண்ணறிவு (Seyarkai Nunnarivu)

#### Task ID: `AI-6-DATA-1.2`
- **Title:** Map the three core AI domains (Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Statistical Data) to human senses.
- **Time Estimate:** 20 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (chart paper, markers)
- **Skill Type:** Analogical Thinking & Mapping
- **Assessment Mode:** Illustrated Poster & Oral Explanation
- **Track Alignment:** Track B
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Maps Computer Vision (CV) to Human Eyes (visual perception).
  - Maps Natural Language Processing (NLP) to Human Ears/Mouth (speech & semantic comprehension).
  - Maps Statistical Data/Data Science to Human Memory & Analytical Brain (finding mathematical patterns in numbers).
  - Gives 1 real Indian application for each domain (e.g., FASTag highway toll = CV, Bhashini speech translation = NLP, monsoon yield prediction = Data).
- **Source Citation:** CBSE Class 6 AI Curriculum, Unit 1, p. 15; NCF-SE C-7.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Human silhouette diagram with empty callout bubbles pointing to eyes, ears, and brain.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Which AI domain is used by a self-driving electric rickshaw navigating an Indian market street? Justify multi-modal fusion."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: कंप्यूटर विज़न (Computer Vision), प्राकृतिक भाषा प्रसंस्करण (Praakritik Bhaasha Prasanskaran - NLP)
  - Telugu: కంప్యూటర్ విజన్ (Computer Vision)

### Sub-Competency C2: Data Collection & Feature Tagging

#### Task ID: `AI-6-DATA-2.1`
- **Title:** Collect and tabulate biometric/botanical leaf traits across 10 fallen leaves in the school garden.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (10 fallen leaves, 15cm ruler, data recording sheet)
- **Skill Type:** Empirical Data Collection & Feature Engineering
- **Assessment Mode:** Data Table & Group Verification
- **Track Alignment:** Track B
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Collects 10 specimen leaves without plucking live flora.
  - Measures and records 4 numerical and categorical features: Length (cm), Width (cm), Margin type (smooth vs serrated), Venation (parallel vs reticulate).
  - Formats data cleanly into rows (samples) and columns (features/attributes).
- **Source Citation:** CBSE Class 6 AI Curriculum, Unit 2: Data Collection, pp. 22–26.
- **Scaffold Support:** Pre-printed spreadsheet grid with feature column headers and margin picture illustrations.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Which single feature among the four is most informative for separating monocot leaves from dicot leaves?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: डेटा संग्रह (Data Sangrah), विशेषताएँ (Viseshtayen - Features), तालिका (Taalika - Table)
  - Bengali: তথ্য সংগ্রহ (Tottho Songroho)

#### Task ID: `AI-6-DATA-2.2`
- **Title:** Clean a noisy dataset by identifying missing values, spelling inconsistencies, and outlier errors.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (printed mock survey dataset of 15 student records)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural Data Wrangling & Precision
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Self-Check
- **Track Alignment:** Track B
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Identifies at least 3 distinct data defects: missing age, inconsistent city spelling ("Delhy" vs "Delhi"), and impossible outlier (Height: "15 meters" instead of "1.5 meters").
  - Corrects errors using logical imputation or flags for removal.
  - Explains the golden rule: "Garbage In, Garbage Out (GIGO)" in machine training.
- **Source Citation:** CBSE Class 6 AI Curriculum, Unit 2, p. 29.
- **Scaffold Support:** Color-coded highlighter guide: Yellow = Missing, Pink = Spelling typo, Blue = Extreme outlier.
- **Extension Challenge:** "How would a smart weather forecast model behave if a sensor reported temperature as $99^\circ\text{C}$ due to a faulty wire?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: डेटा सफाई (Data Safaai - Data Cleaning), विसंगतियां (Visangatiyan - Anomalies)
  - Marathi: डेटा शुद्धीकरण (Data Shuddhikaaran)

### Sub-Competency C3: Manual Classification & Decision Trees

#### Task ID: `AI-6-DATA-3.1`
- **Title:** Construct a binary 20-Questions decision tree to identify 8 Indian animals.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (chart paper, sticky notes or index cards)
- **Skill Type:** Algorithmic Thinking & Logical Branching
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Peer Playtest
- **Track Alignment:** Track B
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Selects 8 distinct Indian animals (e.g., Bengal Tiger, Indian Elephant, Peafowl, King Cobra, Gangetic Dolphin, Camel, Lion-tailed Macaque, Gharial).
  - Drafts hierarchical Yes/No questions (e.g., "Is it an aquatic organism? -> Yes/No").
  - Successfully leads any classmate to the correct animal in 4 questions or fewer.
- **Source Citation:** CBSE Class 6 AI Curriculum, Unit 3: Logic and Algorithms, pp. 35–40.
- **Scaffold Support:** Pre-structured binary tree flowchart template with blank node circles and Yes/No arrow branches.
- **Extension Challenge:** "What question eliminates 50% of the candidate animals immediately? Why do computer scientists call this 'binary search efficiency'?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: निर्णय वृक्ष (Nirnay Vriksh - Decision Tree), द्विआधारी विभाजन (Dwiaadhaari Vibhaajan - Binary Split)
  - Gujarati: નિર્ણય વૃક્ષ (Nirnay Vruksh)

#### Task ID: `AI-6-DATA-3.2`
- **Title:** Simulate a fruit-sorting machine vision classifier using color and texture criteria.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (paper cards depicting apples, bananas, mangoes, and guavas with traits)
- **Skill Type:** Rule Formulation & Simulation
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Simulation
- **Track Alignment:** Track B
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Writes step-by-step pseudo-rules: `IF Color == Yellow AND Shape == Curved THEN Label = Banana`.
  - Tests classifier against 6 unseen fruit cards, including edge cases (e.g., green raw mango vs green guava).
  - Refines rules to resolve misclassifications by adding an extra feature (surface texture: smooth vs bumpy).
- **Source Citation:** CBSE Class 6 AI Curriculum, Unit 3, p. 44.
- **Scaffold Support:** IF-THEN sentence builder cards.
- **Extension Challenge:** "How many distinct features are necessary to guarantee zero error when sorting 10 different types of mangoes?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: वर्गीकरण (Vargeekaran - Classification), छंटाई (Chhantaai - Sorting)
  - Kannada: ವರ್ಗೀಕರಣ (Vargeekarana)

### Sub-Competency C4: Identifying Dataset Bias & Inclusivity

#### Task ID: `AI-6-DATA-4.1`
- **Title:** Audit a photo training set to detect gender, cultural, or geographical representation bias.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (set of 20 printed magazine/stock photos representing 'Doctors', 'Engineers', and 'Chefs')
- **Skill Type:** Critical Sociotechnical Analysis & Ethics
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Audit Report & Classroom Debate
- **Track Alignment:** Track B / Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Tally representations by gender, age, and regional Indian dress.
  - Identifies skew (e.g., 18 out of 20 'Engineers' depicted are young urban males).
  - Explains what happens if an AI hiring system trains exclusively on this skewed photo dataset.
  - Proposes a balanced 20-photo dataset recipe.
- **Source Citation:** CBSE Class 6 AI Curriculum, Unit 2: Data Ethics, pp. 31–34; NCF-SE C-7.2.
- **Scaffold Support:** Diversity tally rubric with checkboxes for demographic representation.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Interview 3 grandparents or relatives about voice assistants failing to understand their regional accents. Propose how Indian speech datasets must be collected."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: डेटा पूर्वाग्रह (Data Poorvaagrah - Dataset Bias), समावेशन (Samaaveshan - Inclusivity)
  - Tamil: தரவு ஒருதலைப்பட்சம் (Taravu Oruthalaippatcham)

#### Task ID: `AI-6-DATA-4.2`
- **Title:** Formulate an 'AI Data Ethics Pledge' for student developers in school projects.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (poster paper, sketch pens)
- **Skill Type:** Ethical Reflection & Civic Communication
- **Assessment Mode:** Peer Review & Group Charter
- **Track Alignment:** Track B
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Formulates 4 core ethical principles: Informed Consent before photographing, Anonymity/Privacy, Balanced Representation, No Harmful Stereotypes.
  - Signs and displays charter in classroom or computer lab.
- **Source Citation:** UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence; CBSE AI Grade 6.
- **Scaffold Support:** Sentence starters: "We promise to never collect data without...", "We will ensure our data includes people from...".
- **Extension Challenge:** "Design a privacy icon system that warns users when an app tracks microphone or camera data in the background."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: नैतिकता प्रतिज्ञा (Naitikta Pratigya), गोपनीयता (Gopaneeyata - Privacy)
  - Malayalam: ഡാറ്റാ ധാർമ്മികത (Data Dhaarmikatha)
