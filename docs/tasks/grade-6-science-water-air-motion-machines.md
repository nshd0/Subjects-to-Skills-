# Grade 6 Science: Water, Air, Motion & Simple Machines
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Science (Middle Stage, Grade 6 - Foundational Bridge)  
**Curriculum Documents:** NCERT Grade 6 Science (Chapter 10: Motion and Measurement of Distances; Chapter 11: Light, Shadows and Reflections; Chapter 14: Water; Chapter 15: Air Around Us); NCF-SE 2023 Middle Stage Science Competencies C-1.1, C-1.2, C-2.1, C-3.1.  
**Focus:** Hands-on observation, low-cost tabletop experiments, local environmental investigation, and transition from sensory observation to scientific inquiry.

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner investigates the properties and cycles of water and air through hands-on inquiry, evaluates community water conservation and atmospheric conditions, measures distances using standardized units while classifying types of motion, and explains how light forms shadows and simple machines multiply mechanical advantage in daily tasks."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **NCERT Grade 6 Science:** Ch 10 (*Motion and Measurement of Distances*), Ch 11 (*Light, Shadows and Reflections*), Ch 14 (*Water*), Ch 15 (*Air Around Us*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Middle Stage Science C-1.1 (Observation & classification of natural matter), C-2.1 (Measurement and units), C-3.1 (Kinematics and physical phenomena), C-4.1 (Ecosystem conservation).
- **CBSE Science Skill Objectives:** S6-01 (Inquiry-based observation), S6-04 (Scientific recording & data tabulation).

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: The Hydrological Cycle & Water Stewardship:** Track the states of water, demonstrate evaporation and condensation, and design rainwater harvesting solutions.
- **C2: Atmospheric Composition & Properties of Air:** Verify that air occupies space, exerts pressure, supports combustion via oxygen, and sustains living organisms.
- **C3: Motion Types & Standardized Measurement:** Measure lengths using SI metric units, transition from non-standard units (cubits, handspans), and classify rectilinear, circular, and periodic motions.
- **C4: Light, Shadows & Simple Mechanical Principles:** Investigate rectilinear propagation of light, construct a pinhole camera, explain shadow variations, and identify simple machines (inclined plane, pulley, lever) that ease work.

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: The Hydrological Cycle & Water Stewardship

#### Task ID: `SCI-6-WATER-1.1`
- **Title:** Build a closed-jar terrarium model demonstrating evaporation, condensation, and precipitation.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (transparent plastic bottle/glass jar, soil, moist pebble, ice cube/film)
- **Skill Type:** Concrete Experimentation & Observation
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Illustrated Log
- **Track Alignment:** Track A (Compulsory Core)
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Assembles closed jar containing damp soil and sealed clear lid.
  - Places ice on top lid or warms base in sunlight to create visible water droplets on underside of lid.
  - Annotates diagram mapping jar droplets to cloud condensation and rainfall.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Science, Chapter 14, pp. 138–141.
- **Scaffold Support:** Step-by-step pictorial assembly card with arrows showing evaporation and condensation.
- **Extension Challenge:** "How does deforestation affect the rate of transpiration in the regional water cycle? Design an experiment with a leafy sprig in a ziplock bag."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: जल चक्र (Jal Chakra), वाष्पीकरण (Vaashpeekaran), संघनन (Sanghanan)
  - Tamil: நீர் சுழற்சி (Neer Suzharchi)

#### Task ID: `SCI-6-WATER-1.2`
- **Title:** Conduct a domestic water audit and calculate daily per-capita water footprint.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min (with prior home tally)
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet, measuring cup / bucket tally)
- **Skill Type:** Data Collection & Environmental Analysis
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Family Action Plan
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Logs daily bucket usage across 5 activities (drinking, cooking, bathing, washing utensils, flushing).
  - Calculates total family liters and divides by number of members.
  - Compares result against the WHO standard of 50–100 liters/person/day.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Science, Chapter 14, p. 136.
- **Scaffold Support:** Pre-formatted tally chart with standard bucket volumes (1 bucket = 15 liters).
- **Extension Challenge:** "Calculate how much water could be saved annually in your apartment/street by installing aerator taps that reduce flow by 30%."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: जल संरक्षण (Jal Sanrakshan), प्रति व्यक्ति खपत (Prati Vyakti Khapat)
  - Telugu: నీటి పరిరక్షణ (Neeti Parirakshana)

#### Task ID: `SCI-6-WATER-1.3`
- **Title:** Design a rooftop rainwater harvesting schematic for a rural or urban school.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (drawing paper, colored pencils)
- **Skill Type:** Applied Design & Problem Solving
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task / Rubric
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Draws catchment surface (roof), conveyance gutters, first-flush diverter, and sand-gravel filter pit.
  - Labels storage sump and recharge well for groundwater replenishment.
  - Explains why the first 15 minutes of rainfall must be diverted away from storage.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Science, Chapter 14, p. 144; NCF-SE Science C-4.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Template outline of a house roof and underground tank with blank labels.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Given a roof area of $100\text{ m}^2$ and annual rainfall of $800\text{ mm}$, calculate potential harvest volume assuming 80% collection efficiency."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: वर्षा जल संचयन (Varsha Jal Sanchayan), भूजल पुनर्भरण (Bhoojal Punarbharan)
  - Kannada: ಮಳೆನೀರು ಕೊಯ್ಲು (Maleneeru Koylu)

### Sub-Competency C2: Atmospheric Composition & Properties of Air

#### Task ID: `SCI-6-AIR-2.1`
- **Title:** Invert a drinking glass in a bowl of water to prove that air occupies space.
- **Time Estimate:** 20 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (transparent tumbler, tub of water, dry paper tissue)
- **Skill Type:** Hands-on Inquiry & Observation
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Demonstration & Explanation
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Stuffs dry tissue at bottom of tumbler and pushes vertically into water; proves tissue remains dry because trapped air prevents water entry.
  - Tilts tumbler slightly to observe escaping air bubbles and water displacement.
  - Explains deduction: Air is matter that takes up physical space.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Science, Chapter 15, p. 148.
- **Scaffold Support:** Observation prompt: "What happened to the tissue? What came out when you tilted the glass?"
- **Extension Challenge:** "Design a setup using a plastic syringe with no needle to demonstrate the compressibility of air versus water."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: वायु स्थान घेरती है (Vaayu Sthaan Gherti Hai), विस्थापन (Visthaapan)
  - Marathi: हवा जागा व्यापते (Hawa Jaaga Vyaapte)

#### Task ID: `SCI-6-AIR-2.2`
- **Title:** Investigate the role of oxygen in combustion using burning candles and glass tumblers.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (teacher-supervised candle, 2 different sized tumblers, matchbox)
- **Skill Type:** Controlled Experimentation & Timing
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Data Table
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Records candle burn duration under small tumbler vs large tumbler.
  - Confirms candle extinguishes when oxygen inside tumbler is depleted.
  - Concludes that volume of trapped air directly determines burning time.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Science, Chapter 15, p. 150.
- **Scaffold Support:** Teacher safety checklist and stop-clock recording sheet.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Why does water rise inside an inverted tumbler placed over an extinguished candle? Critique the common misconception that oxygen volume loss causes the entire water rise."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: दहन (Dahan - Combustion), ऑक्सीजन (Oxygen), वायुमंडलीय दबाव (Vaayumandaliya Dabaav)
  - Bengali: দহন (Dahon), অক্সিজেন (Oxygen)

#### Task ID: `SCI-6-AIR-2.3`
- **Title:** Construct a paper pinwheel (Firki) and calibrate it to detect wind direction and speed.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (square paper sheet, pin, stick/straw, scissors)
- **Skill Type:** Maker Skill & Environmental Observation
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Field Note
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Folds and pins a 4-vane pinwheel that spins freely with minimal friction.
  - Tests rotation outdoors facing different directions to locate prevailing wind.
  - Correlates rotational speed with physical indicators (tree leaves rustling vs dust blowing).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Science, Chapter 15, p. 147.
- **Scaffold Support:** Origami folding guide with safety pin insertion guidelines.
- **Extension Challenge:** "How do modern wind turbines use aerodynamic lift rather than simple flat blades to generate electrical energy?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: फिरकी (Firki), पवन चक्की (Pawan Chakki), वायु दिशा (Vaayu Disha)
  - Gujarati: ફિરકી (Firki), પવન ઊર્જા (Pawan Oorja)

### Sub-Competency C3: Motion Types & Standardized Measurement

#### Task ID: `SCI-6-MOTION-3.1`
- **Title:** Compare body-part measurements (cubit, handspan) against meter scale to prove need for standard units.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (meter tape / 30cm ruler, classroom bench)
- **Skill Type:** Comparative Data Collection & Critical Thinking
- **Assessment Mode:** Peer Review & Group Comparison
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Measures teacher table using handspans of 3 different classmates; notes variance.
  - Measures same table using calibrated meter scale; notes invariant result.
  - Writes a 3-sentence justification for why international commerce requires standard SI units.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Science, Chapter 10, pp. 98–101; NCF-SE Science C-2.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Conversion table showing historical units (balisht/handspan, gaj/yard, haath/cubit).
- **Extension Challenge:** "Research the history of the French metric system (1790) and the definition of 1 meter based on the speed of light."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: मानक मात्रक (Maanak Maatrak - Standard Unit), बालिश्त (Balisht), हाथ (Haath)
  - Tamil: நிலையான அலகு (Nilaiyaana Alagu)

#### Task ID: `SCI-6-MOTION-3.2`
- **Title:** Classify everyday moving objects into rectilinear, circular, and periodic motions.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (video clips or playground observation, worksheet)
- **Skill Type:** Conceptual Categorization
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Matrix Table
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Classifies at least 6 phenomena (e.g., falling stone = rectilinear, ceiling fan = circular, pendulum/swing = periodic, rolling ball = combined).
  - Justifies classification based on object's trajectory over time.
  - Identifies at least one object demonstrating simultaneous circular and rectilinear motion (bicycle wheel).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Science, Chapter 10, pp. 104–106.
- **Scaffold Support:** Matrix chart with definitions: "Straight path? Fixed center pivot? Repeats at fixed intervals?"
- **Extension Challenge:** "Describe the motion of the tip of the minute hand of a clock versus the clock pendulum over a 24-hour cycle."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: सरल रेखीय गति (Saral Rekhiya Gati), वर्तुल गति (Vartul Gati), आवर्ती गति (Aavarti Gati)
  - Malayalam: നേർരേഖാ ചലനം (Ner-rekha Chalanam), വൃത്തചലനം (Vritta Chalanam)

#### Task ID: `SCI-6-MOTION-3.3`
- **Title:** Measure length of a curved boundary (leaf edge or river on map) using a thread and meter ruler.
- **Time Estimate:** 20 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (cotton thread, fresh tree leaf, 15cm ruler)
- **Skill Type:** Precision Measurement Technique
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Traces thread snugly along perimeter of leaf without stretching.
  - Marks start and end points on thread with ink.
  - Straightens thread along ruler to measure total perimeter in centimeters and millimeters.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Science, Chapter 10, p. 103.
- **Scaffold Support:** Demonstration video clip or teacher table exemplar.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Can this thread method be adapted to measure the coastline of India on an atlas? Discuss the coastline paradox."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: वक्र रेखा (Vakra Rekha), परिमाप (Parimaap)
  - Odia: ବକ୍ର ରେଖା (Bakra Rekha)

### Sub-Competency C4: Light, Shadows & Simple Mechanical Principles

#### Task ID: `SCI-6-LIGHT-4.1`
- **Title:** Build a cardboard pinhole camera and observe the inverted image of an outdoor tree/building.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (two cardboard boxes fitting inside one another, tracing paper, pin, black tape)
- **Skill Type:** Fabrication & Optical Principle Demonstration
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Sketch
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Assembles sliding boxes with tiny pinhole at front and tracing paper screen at rear.
  - Focuses on bright outdoor object under dark cloth/hood.
  - Sketches observed inverted image and explains why light traveling in straight lines creates inversion.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Science, Chapter 11, pp. 110–112.
- **Scaffold Support:** Ray diagram showing light rays from top and bottom of object crossing through pinhole.
- **Extension Challenge:** "What happens if you enlarge the pinhole with a nail? Explain why image becomes brighter but blurred."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: सूचीछिद्र कैमरा (Soocheechhidra Camera), उल्टा प्रतिबिंब (Ulta Pratibimb), ऋजुरेखीय संचरण (Rijurekhiya Sancharan)
  - Punjabi: ਸੂਚੀ-ਛੇਦ ਕੈਮਰਾ (Soochi-Chhed Camera)

#### Task ID: `SCI-6-LIGHT-4.2`
- **Title:** Track shadow length and orientation variations throughout the day using an outdoor gnomon stick.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min (measurements at 9 AM, 12 Noon, 3 PM)
- **Resource Level:** Low (vertical 30cm stick fixed in playground ground, chalk, measuring tape)
- **Skill Type:** Longitudinal Observation & Astronomical Linkage
- **Assessment Mode:** Field Data Sheet & Graph
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Records shadow length and compass direction at minimum 3 intervals during the day.
  - Observes that shadow is shortest at solar noon when sun is at highest elevation.
  - Identifies 3 prerequisites for shadow formation: light source, opaque object, and screen/ground.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Science, Chapter 11, pp. 108–109.
- **Scaffold Support:** Pre-chalked compass circle on playground ground with marked cardinal points.
- **Extension Challenge:** "How did ancient Indian astronomers use sundials (Samrat Yantra at Jantar Mantar) to measure time to within two seconds?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: छाया (Chhaya - Shadow), अपारदर्शी (Apaardarshee - Opaque), धूपघड़ी (Dhoopghadi - Sundial)
  - Marathi: सावली (Saavli), सूर्यघड्याळ (Sooryaghadyal)

#### Task ID: `SCI-6-LIGHT-4.3`
- **Title:** Test how an inclined plane and pulley reduce the effort required to lift a school bag.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (spring balance or rubber band scale, wooden plank / cardboard ramp, heavy book/bag)
- **Skill Type:** Hands-on Physics & Mechanical Advantage
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Written Response
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Measures force required to lift bag vertically versus dragging it up a smooth wooden ramp.
  - Records that vertical lift requires greater force while ramp trades smaller force over greater distance.
  - Identifies 3 inclined planes in everyday life (wheelchair ramps, screw threads, winding hill roads).
- **Source Citation:** NCF-SE 2023 Middle Stage Science C-3.1; NCERT Applied Mechanics.
- **Scaffold Support:** Visual force diagram showing effort versus load distance.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Calculate mechanical advantage $\text{MA} = \frac{\text{Load}}{\text{Effort}}$ for two ramps of different slopes ($30^\circ$ vs $60^\circ$)."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: नत समतल (Nat Samtal - Inclined Plane), घिरनी (Ghirnee - Pulley), यांत्रिक लाभ (Yaantrik Laabh)
  - Telugu: వాలుతలం (Vaalutalam), కప్పి (Kappi)
