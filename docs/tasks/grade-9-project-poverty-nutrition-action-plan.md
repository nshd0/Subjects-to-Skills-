# Multidisciplinary Project Blueprint (Grade 9)
## "Nutrition Security & Civic Action Plan: Statistical Modeling, Caloric Calculations & Constitutional Rights"

**Target Grade Band:** Secondary Stage (Grade 9, Ages 14–15 - Foundation Depth)  
**Curricular Integration:** Science (Cellular Nutrition & Energy) + Mathematics (Statistics & Polynomial Modeling) + AI (Dataset Bias & Scoping) + Social Science (Economics of Poverty & Constitutional Rights)  
**Duration:** 3–4 Weeks (Integrated across 14 class periods)  
**Real-World Context:** Malnutrition and hidden hunger persist across marginalized rural and urban settlements despite national surplus food grain stocks. Addressing nutrition requires statistical rigor, biological understanding of cellular metabolism, fair algorithmic food rationing, and constitutional accountability.

---

## 1. Project Challenge Statement

> *"How can we, as youth policy researchers and civic scientists, analyze local household dietary datasets using statistical distributions, calculate cellular nutritional deficits, audit automated public food distribution models for demographic bias, and formulate a constitutional 'Nutrition Security Action Masterplan' under Article 21 to eradicate local child stunting?"*

## 3. Project Phase Breakdown & Workflow

```
[ Phase 1: Dietary Data Collection & Statistical Profiling ] (Week 1)
  ├── Conduct dietary survey across 30 mock diverse households (SOC-9-POV-3.1)
  ├── Convert food grains to daily kilocalorie totals (SCI-9-MATT-1.1)
  └── Draw grouped histograms and calculate Median caloric intake (MATH-9-STAT-4.1, 4.2)

[ Phase 2: Biological Diagnosis & Algorithmic Audit ] (Week 2)
  ├── Model micronutrient cell absorption and anemia consequences (SCI-9-CELL-2.2)
  ├── Audit biometric smart ration card algorithms for biometric exclusion (AI-9-BIAS-1.1)
  └── Construct Explainable AI transparency rules for ration shops (AI-9-XAI-4.1)

[ Phase 3: Policy Synthesis & Constitutional Advocacy ] (Week 3-4)
  ├── Review Public Distribution System (PDS) and mid-day meal scheme efficacy (SOC-9-POV-4.1)
  ├── Ground community nutrition entitlement in Article 21 Right to Life (SOC-9-CONST-2.1)
  └── Deliver "School & Ward Nutrition Security Action Plan" to District Food Controller
```

---

## 4. Final Cumulative Deliverables

1. **Statistical Nutrition & Calorie Report:**
   - A complete statistical dossier containing grouped frequency histograms, mean vs median comparisons, and income-to-nutrition correlation graphs.
2. **Algorithmic Fairness & Model Card Brief:**
   - A formal Model Card ensuring biometric PDS authentication includes offline manual exception overrides for manual laborers whose fingerprints are eroded.
3. **Citizen Nutrition Charter & Policy Brief:**
   - A 4-page evidence-based civic brief submitted to the District Magistrate recommending decentralized millets procurement, fortified school meals, and enhanced PDS allocations.
