# Grade 6 Mathematics: Ratios, Integers & Foundational Geometry
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Mathematics (Middle Stage, Grade 6 - Foundational Bridge)  
**Curriculum Documents:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics (Chapter 4: Basic Geometrical Ideas; Chapter 6: Integers; Chapter 12: Ratio and Proportion); NCF-SE 2023 Middle Stage Mathematics Competencies C-1.1, C-1.2, C-2.1.  
**Focus:** Concrete-to-abstract transition using physical manipulatives, number lines, real-life ratios, and geometric sketches.

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner understands and applies the concepts of ratios and proportions to compare quantities in everyday contexts, represents and performs directed operations with integers using concrete models and number lines, and identifies, constructs, and classifies basic geometric figures (points, lines, angles, triangles, and quadrilaterals) from physical objects."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics:** Ch 4 (*Basic Geometrical Ideas*), Ch 6 (*Integers*), Ch 12 (*Ratio and Proportion*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Middle Stage Math C-1.1 (Number systems & negative numbers as directed magnitude), C-1.2 (Proportional reasoning), C-3.1 (Spatial understanding and geometric shapes).
- **CBSE Competency Benchmark:** Middle Stage Math Proficiency Benchmark M6-02, M6-05, M6-08.

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: Proportional Reasoning & Ratios:** Understand ratio as a comparison of two quantities of the same unit through division; simplify ratios and solve real-life sharing problems.
- **C2: Directed Numbers & Integer Operations:** Represent positive and negative numbers as opposites in real life (elevation, temperature, profit/loss); position on a number line; perform addition and subtraction.
- **C3: Spatial & Basic Geometrical Constructions:** Recognize and draw points, line segments, rays, intersecting and parallel lines; identify angles and measure types (acute, right, obtuse).
- **C4: Polygons & Real-World Geometrical Classification:** Classify closed 2D figures, triangles by sides and angles, and identify elements of circles (center, radius, diameter, chord) in local architecture.

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: Proportional Reasoning & Ratios

#### Task ID: `MATH-6-RATIO-1.1`
- **Title:** Measure classroom dimensions and express length-to-width ratios in simplest form.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (meter tape / 30cm ruler, notebook)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural Skill & Concrete Modeling
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Written Response
- **Track Alignment:** Track A (Compulsory Core)
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Measures at least 2 rectangular surfaces (e.g., student desk, blackboard, room floor).
  - Calculates ratio using matching units (cm or meters).
  - Simplifies the ratio to lowest terms (e.g., 120 cm : 60 cm = 2:1).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics, Chapter 12, p. 245; NCF-SE 2023 Math C-1.2.
- **Scaffold Support:** Provide a pre-printed measurement table with unit conversion reminders (1 m = 100 cm) and simplified factor guide.
- **Extension Challenge:** "If the desk length increases by 50%, what must the new width be to maintain the exact same ratio?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: अनुपात (Anupaat), सरलतम रूप (Saraltam Roop)
  - Tamil: விகிதம் (Vigitham) / Marathi: गुणोत्तर (Gunottar)

#### Task ID: `MATH-6-RATIO-1.2`
- **Title:** Calculate recipe scaling ratios for a mid-day meal serving 10 vs 50 students.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (pencil, chart paper)
- **Skill Type:** Conceptual Understanding & Real-World Application
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Peer Review
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Correctly calculates scaling factor (50 / 10 = 5).
  - Scales 4 ingredients (rice, dal, salt, vegetables) proportionally without altering ratio.
  - Explains why adding 40 grams to each ingredient fails proportional scaling.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics, Chapter 12, p. 251.
- **Scaffold Support:** Provide unitary method template: "Quantity for 10 students -> Quantity for 1 student -> Quantity for 50 students."
- **Extension Challenge:** "If dal prices rise by 20% and budget remains fixed, determine the adjusted rice-to-dal ratio required to feed 50 students."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: समानुपात (Samanupaat), ऐकिक नियम (Aikik Niyam)
  - Kannada: ಅನುಪಾತ (Anupaatha)

#### Task ID: `MATH-6-RATIO-1.3`
- **Title:** Test pairs of ratios for equivalence using cross-multiplication.
- **Time Estimate:** 20 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet, blackboard)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural Skill
- **Assessment Mode:** Self-Check & Written Response
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Evaluates at least 4 ratio pairs (e.g., 2:3 and 6:9; 4:5 and 12:20).
  - Demonstrates cross-product equality rule ($a \times d = b \times c$).
  - Correctly labels each pair as "in proportion" or "not in proportion".
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics, Chapter 12, p. 254.
- **Scaffold Support:** Visual balance beam diagram showing cross-multiplication balance.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Find the missing term $x$ in $3:7 :: 15:x$ and explain its geometric interpretation."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: चरम पद (Charam Pad), मध्य पद (Madhya Pad)
  - Bengali: অনুপাত ও সমানুপাত (Anupat o Samanupat)

### Sub-Competency C2: Directed Numbers & Integer Operations

#### Task ID: `MATH-6-INT-2.1`
- **Title:** Map daily life opposites onto a horizontal and vertical integer number line.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (chalk, playground floor or notebook)
- **Skill Type:** Conceptual Understanding & Visual Representation
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Oral Explanation
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Identifies at least 4 opposite pairs (above/below sea level, profit/loss, temperature above/below $0^\circ\text{C}$, steps forward/backward).
  - Positions $0$ correctly as reference datum.
  - Places positive numbers to right/up and negative numbers to left/down with uniform spacing.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics, Chapter 6, p. 115; NCF-SE 2023 Math C-1.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Draw a thermometer model with marked zero line.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Can negative numbers have magnitude larger than positive numbers? Explain difference between value and absolute value."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: पूर्णांक (Poornaank), ऋणात्मक (Rinaatmak), धनात्मक (Dhanaatmak)
  - Telugu: పూర్ణాంకాలు (Poornaankaalu)

#### Task ID: `MATH-6-INT-2.2`
- **Title:** Simulate integer addition and subtraction using red/blue token counters.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (two-color paper tokens or bottle caps)
- **Skill Type:** Concrete Modeling & Procedural Skill
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Peer Review
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Uses Blue = $+1$ and Red = $-1$; demonstrates zero pairs ($+1 + (-1) = 0$).
  - Solves $+5 + (-3)$ by pairing and removing 3 zero pairs to find $+2$.
  - Solves $-4 - (-2)$ and $-3 - (+2)$ using chip modeling.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics, Chapter 6, p. 124.
- **Scaffold Support:** Physical two-sided counter mat with a designated "Zero Pair Trash Bin".
- **Extension Challenge:** "Formulate a general rule for subtracting a negative number without using physical chips."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: योज्य प्रतिलोम (Yojya Pratilom - Additive Inverse)
  - Gujarati: પૂર્ણાંક સંખ્યાઓ (Poornaank Sankhyao)

#### Task ID: `MATH-6-INT-2.3`
- **Title:** Solve multi-step integer word problems involving elevation and bank balances.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet)
- **Skill Type:** Critical Thinking & Application
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Writes arithmetic integer expressions from text prompts.
  - Correctly evaluates net balance (e.g., balance ₹500, withdrawal ₹750 = ₹-250 overdraft).
  - States answer with appropriate signs and physical meaning.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics, Chapter 6, p. 130.
- **Scaffold Support:** Three-step graphic organizer: "Starting point -> Operation (+/-) -> Ending point".
- **Extension Challenge:** "A submarine descends at 15 m/min from sea level. Write an integer formula for its depth after $t$ minutes."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: जमा (Jama - Credit), निकासी (Nikaasi - Debit)
  - Odia: ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣସଂଖ୍ୟା (Purnasankhya)

### Sub-Competency C3: Spatial & Basic Geometrical Constructions

#### Task ID: `MATH-6-GEOM-3.1`
- **Title:** Differentiate and sketch points, line segments, lines, and rays from classroom architecture.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (ruler, pencil, geometry sheet)
- **Skill Type:** Conceptual Understanding & Drawing
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Sketch
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Identifies at least 2 real examples of each (e.g., ray = laser/torchlight, line segment = table edge).
  - Uses standard mathematical notation ($\overline{AB}$, $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$, $\vec{AB}$).
  - Explains why a line has no endpoints while a ray has one.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics, Chapter 4, p. 69; NCF-SE 2023 Math C-3.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Visual flashcard guide with endpoint dots and directional arrows.
- **Extension Challenge:** "How many distinct line segments can be formed by joining 5 non-collinear points? Prove your answer."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: बिंदु (Bindu), रेखाखंड (Rekhakhand), किरण (Kiran), रेखा (Rekha)
  - Malayalam: ബിന്ദു (Bindu), രേഖാഖണ്ഡം (Rekhakhandam)

#### Task ID: `MATH-6-GEOM-3.2`
- **Title:** Measure and classify door swing angles into acute, right, obtuse, and straight angles.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (cardboard protractor / angle maker strips)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural Skill & Kinesthetic Learning
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Builds a simple two-strip cardboard angle-maker with split pin.
  - Measures door opening at 3 different positions.
  - Classifies each angle correctly relative to $90^\circ$ and $180^\circ$.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics, Chapter 5, p. 89.
- **Scaffold Support:** L-corner card for quick $90^\circ$ right-angle benchmark test.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Estimate the angle between clock hands at 3:15 PM and 8:20 PM; explain why 3:15 PM is not exactly $0^\circ$."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: न्यून कोण (Nyun Kon), समकोण (Samkon), अधिक कोण (Adhik Kon), ऋजु कोण (Riju Kon)
  - Punjabi: ਨਿਊਨ ਕੋਣ (Nyun Kon), ਸਮਕੋਣ (Samkon)

#### Task ID: `MATH-6-GEOM-3.3`
- **Title:** Trace and verify parallel and intersecting lines on a local street map or classroom layout.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (ruler, map printout or graph paper)
- **Skill Type:** Critical Thinking & Spatial Analysis
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Peer Review
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Identifies 2 pairs of parallel lines and explains why they never meet even if extended.
  - Identifies perpendicular intersecting lines ($90^\circ$).
  - Explains safety implications of right-angle vs acute-angle road junctions.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics, Chapter 4, p. 73.
- **Scaffold Support:** Highlighting parallel edges with colored markers.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Can two lines be non-parallel and non-intersecting in 3D space? Explore skew lines using classroom corners."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: समांतर रेखाएं (Samantar Rekhaen), प्रतिच्छेदी रेखाएं (Pratichhedi Rekhaen)
  - Assamese: সমান্তৰাল ৰেখা (Samantaral Rekha)

### Sub-Competency C4: Polygons & Real-World Geometrical Classification

#### Task ID: `MATH-6-POLY-4.1`
- **Title:** Construct and classify triangles using toothpicks/straws based on sides and angles.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (toothpicks or dried broomsticks, clay/glue)
- **Skill Type:** Hands-on Construction & Classification
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Exhibition
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Constructs equilateral, isosceles, and scalene triangles.
  - Tests triangle inequality rule (sum of two sides > third side) with straws of lengths 3cm, 4cm, 8cm to prove impossibility.
  - Labels angle types for each constructed triangle.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics, Chapter 5, p. 101.
- **Scaffold Support:** Pre-cut straws labeled with lengths in centimeters.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Can a triangle have two right angles or two obtuse angles? Justify using the angle sum theorem."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: समबाहु (Sambahu), समद्विबाहु (Samdwibahu), विषमबाहु (Vishambahu) त्रिभुज
  - Bengali: সমবাহু ত্রিভুজ (Samabahu Tribhuj)

#### Task ID: `MATH-6-POLY-4.2`
- **Title:** Deconstruct circle components using a thread, chalk, and circular container lids.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (thread, bottle lids, chalk/pencil)
- **Skill Type:** Conceptual Understanding & Measurement
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Diagram
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Traces a circular boundary; marks center, radius, diameter, and chord.
  - Verifies that diameter = $2 \times \text{radius}$.
  - Demonstrates that diameter is the longest possible chord.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics, Chapter 4, p. 82.
- **Scaffold Support:** Diagram template with color-coded callouts for radius, chord, and arc.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Wrap thread around lid circumference, measure length, and divide by diameter. Compare ratio across 3 lids."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: त्रिज्या (Trijya - Radius), व्यास (Vyaas - Diameter), जीवा (Jeeva - Chord), परिधि (Paridhi - Circumference)
  - Marathi: त्रिज्या (Trijya), व्यास (Vyas)

#### Task ID: `MATH-6-POLY-4.3`
- **Title:** Create a geometric tessellation tile pattern using regular polygons found in Indian craft.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (paper, scissors, colored sketch pens)
- **Skill Type:** Creative Application & Spatial Reasoning
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task / Rubric
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Cuts identical triangles or quadrilaterals and tessellates without gaps or overlaps.
  - Explains why shapes fit together at vertices without leaving spaces.
  - Connects pattern to Indian floor art (Rangoli/Kolam) or architectural jalis.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Mathematics, Chapter 5, p. 106; NCF-SE Art & Math Integration.
- **Scaffold Support:** Grid paper template for equilateral triangle grid.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Explain mathematically why regular pentagons cannot tile a flat floor without gaps."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: बहुभुज (Bahubhuj), समरूपता (Samroopta), रंगोली पैटर्न (Rangoli Pattern)
  - Tamil: பலகோணம் (Palakonam)
