# Grade 7 Mathematics: Integers, Fractions & Simple Equations
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Mathematics (Middle Stage, Grade 7 - Core Cross-Disciplinary)  
**Curriculum Documents:** NCERT Grade 7 Mathematics (Chapter 1: Integers; Chapter 2: Fractions and Decimals; Chapter 4: Simple Equations); NCF-SE 2023 Middle Stage Mathematics Competencies C-1.1, C-1.2, C-2.1.  
**Focus:** Middle stage core rigor: Transitioning from arithmetic computation to algebraic modeling, balance equations, fractional rates, and integer product rules.

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner performs operations on integers and rational fractions across multi-step contextual problems, models physical and financial phenomena into simple linear equations with one variable, and solves equations using systematic balancing and transposition while interpreting solutions in real-world situations."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **NCERT Grade 7 Mathematics:** Ch 1 (*Integers*), Ch 2 (*Fractions and Decimals*), Ch 4 (*Simple Equations*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Middle Stage Math C-1.1 (Signed numbers & algebraic structures), C-1.2 (Fractional & proportional calculations), C-2.1 (Equation formulation & solving).
- **CBSE Learning Outcomes:** M7-01, M7-02, M7-04.

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: Advanced Integer Operations & Properties:** Multiply and divide signed integers; apply commutative, associative, and distributive properties to simplify expressions.
- **C2: Rational Fractions & Decimal Quantities:** Perform multiplication and division of proper/improper fractions; solve multi-step recipe, area, and speed rate problems.
- **C3: Formulating Simple Equations from Situations:** Translate verbal real-world statements involving unknown ages, geometric dimensions, and financial balances into algebraic balance equations ($ax + b = c$).
- **C4: Solving Equations via Balancing & Transposition:** Execute systematic inverse operations, isolate variables, verify roots by substitution, and resolve real-world dilemmas.

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: Advanced Integer Operations & Properties

#### Task ID: `MATH-7-INT-1.1`
- **Title:** Prove integer multiplication sign rules using number line jumping patterns and physical grid modeling.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (chalk, number line sheet)
- **Skill Type:** Conceptual Understanding & Inductive Reasoning
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Written Derivation
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Demonstrates $(+a) \times (-b) = -(ab)$ as repeated backward steps on a number line.
  - Proves $(-a) \times (-b) = +(ab)$ using pattern continuation: $(-3) \times 2 = -6, (-3) \times 1 = -3, (-3) \times 0 = 0, (-3) \times (-1) = +3$.
  - Articulates why multiplying two negative numbers must yield a positive product.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Mathematics, Chapter 1, pp. 10–14.
- **Scaffold Support:** Pattern table starting from $(+3) \times (+3)$ down through negative multiplier steps.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Determine the sign of the product of 99 negative integers and 2 positive integers. Justify with a general parity theorem."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: गुणन संक्रिया (Gunan Sankriya), गुणन का क्रमविनिमेय नियम (Gunan ka Kramvinimey Niyam)
  - Tamil: முழு எண்களின் பெருக்கல் (Muzhu Engalin Perukkal)

#### Task ID: `MATH-7-INT-1.2`
- **Title:** Apply the distributive property of multiplication over addition to compute large mental calculations.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet, timed mental math flashcards)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural Efficiency & Number Sense
- **Assessment Mode:** Self-Check & Timed Task
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Expresses complex calculations using distributive law: $a \times (b + c) = (a \times b) + (a \times c)$ (e.g., $18 \times 98 = 18 \times (100 - 2)$).
  - Evaluates expressions with negative integers: $(-25) \times 102 = (-25) \times (100 + 2) = -2500 - 50 = -2550$.
  - Solves at least 5 calculations mentally without traditional column multiplication.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Mathematics, Chapter 1, pp. 16–19.
- **Scaffold Support:** Step-by-step bracket expansion graphic organizer.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Prove that division is not distributive over addition: Does $a \div (b + c) = (a \div b) + (a \div c)$ hold? Give a counter-example."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: वितरण नियम (Vitaran Niyam - Distributive Property)
  - Kannada: ವಿಭಾಜಕ ಗುಣ (Vibhaajaka Guna)

### Sub-Competency C2: Rational Fractions & Decimal Quantities

#### Task ID: `MATH-7-FRAC-2.1`
- **Title:** Model fraction multiplication using folded paper grids and overlapping shaded areas.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (square paper sheets, colored pencils)
- **Skill Type:** Concrete Visual Modeling
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Illustrated Sheet
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Folds paper horizontally into 3 parts and shades 2 parts ($\frac{2}{3}$).
  - Folds paper vertically into 4 parts and shades 3 parts ($\frac{3}{4}$).
  - Identifies double-shaded overlapping rectangles ($\frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$).
  - Derives formal rule: $\frac{\text{Numerator } \times \text{ Numerator}}{\text{Denominator } \times \text{ Denominator}}$.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Mathematics, Chapter 2, pp. 33–37.
- **Scaffold Support:** Transparent plastic grid overlays for visual inspection.
- **Extension Challenge:** "How does multiplying a number by a proper fraction ($\frac{1}{2}$) compare with multiplying by an improper fraction ($\frac{5}{4}$) in terms of product magnitude?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: भिन्न (Bhinn - Fraction), उचित भिन्न (Uchit Bhinn - Proper Fraction), विषम भिन्न (Visham Bhinn - Improper Fraction)
  - Telugu: భిన్నాలు (Bhinnaalu)

#### Task ID: `MATH-7-FRAC-2.2`
- **Title:** Solve real-world reciprocal and fraction division problems in cooking and tailoring.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet, tailoring tape / ruler)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural & Applied Problem Solving
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Defines reciprocal of a fraction ($\frac{a}{b} \rightarrow \frac{b}{a}$).
  - Solves division by converting to multiplication of reciprocal: $\frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{d}{c}$.
  - Solves: "A tailor has $7\frac{1}{2}\text{ meters}$ of ribbon; each school badge requires $\frac{3}{4}\text{ meters}$. How many badges can be cut?" (Answer: 10 badges).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Mathematics, Chapter 2, pp. 41–45.
- **Scaffold Support:** Visual ribbon strip segmenting diagram.
- **Extension Challenge:** "A water pump fills $\frac{4}{5}$ of a tank in $1\frac{1}{3}$ hours. How many hours will it take to fill the entire tank?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: व्युत्क्रम (Vyutkram - Reciprocal), भिन्न का भाग (Bhinn ka Bhaag)
  - Marathi: गुणाकार व्यस्त (Gunakar Vyast)

### Sub-Competency C3: Formulating Simple Equations from Situations

#### Task ID: `MATH-7-EQN-3.1`
- **Title:** Translate word puzzles into algebraic balance statements with one unknown variable.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet, blackboard)
- **Skill Type:** Mathematical Modeling & Language Translation
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Peer Verification
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Identifies the unknown quantity and assigns an algebraic variable ($x, y, m$).
  - Expresses operations accurately (e.g., "Take away 7 from 5 times a number gives 43" -> $5x - 7 = 43$).
  - Formulates at least 5 equations from distinct real-world contexts (ages, perimeter, shopping).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Mathematics, Chapter 4, pp. 77–81; NCF-SE Math C-2.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Translation dictionary: "Added to" -> $+$; "Less than" -> $-$; "Times/Product" -> $\times$; "Is/Gives" -> $=$.
- **Extension Challenge:** "A rectangular vegetable garden has perimeter 56m. Its length is 4m more than twice its breadth. Formulate the single-variable equation."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: सरल समीकरण (Saral Sameekaran), अज्ञात राशि (Agyaat Raashi - Variable)
  - Bengali: সরল সমীকরণ (Saral Samikaran)

### Sub-Competency C4: Solving Equations via Balancing & Transposition

#### Task ID: `MATH-7-EQN-4.1`
- **Title:** Solve linear equations using a physical two-pan balance scale model.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (two-pan kitchen balance or cardboard coat-hanger scale, weights/marbles)
- **Skill Type:** Concrete Modeling & Equation Balancing
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Places mystery pouch ($x$) and 4 pebbles on left pan, balancing 11 pebbles on right pan ($x + 4 = 11$).
  - Demonstrates that removing 4 pebbles from BOTH pans preserves equilibrium ($x = 7$).
  - Connects balancing rule to formal algebraic property: "Whatever operation you perform on LHS, you must perform identically on RHS."
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Mathematics, Chapter 4, pp. 83–86.
- **Scaffold Support:** Visual balance beam diagram showing balanced pans with equal weights removed.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Model and solve an equation with coefficients: $3x + 2 = 14$ using the balance scale."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: तुला संतुलन विधि (Tula Santulan Vidhi), वाम पक्ष (LHS), दक्षिण पक्ष (RHS)
  - Gujarati: સંતુલન પદ્ધતિ (Santulan Paddhati)

#### Task ID: `MATH-7-EQN-4.2`
- **Title:** Solve multi-step equations via transposition and verify roots through LHS/RHS substitution.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural Accuracy & Rigorous Verification
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Self-Check
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Solves equations requiring transposition: $4(m + 3) = 18$ or $3p/4 = 6$.
  - Reverses operations correctly (addition becomes subtraction when crossing equals sign; multiplication becomes division).
  - Substitutes calculated root back into original equation to confirm $\text{LHS} = \text{RHS}$.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Mathematics, Chapter 4, pp. 86–90.
- **Scaffold Support:** Color-coded transposition path cards showing operation flips ($+ \leftrightarrow -$, $\times \leftrightarrow \div$).
- **Extension Challenge:** "Solve $\frac{x}{2} + \frac{x}{3} = 10$ by clearing denominators with LCM."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: पक्षांतरण (Pakshaantaran - Transposition), मूल का सत्यापन (Mool ka Satyaapan - Root Verification)
  - Malayalam: പക്ഷാന്തരണം (Pakshaantaranam)
