# Grade 9 Science: Matter, Cell Biology & Newton's Laws of Motion
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Science (Secondary Stage, Grade 9 - Foundation Depth)  
**Curriculum Documents:** NCERT Grade 9 Science (Chapter 1: Matter in Our Surroundings; Chapter 5: The Fundamental Unit of Life; Chapter 9: Force and Laws of Motion); NCF-SE 2023 Secondary Stage Science Competencies C-1.1, C-2.1, C-3.1.  
**Focus:** Microscopic cellular structures, particulate kinetic theory, thermodynamic phase transitions, and quantitative Newtonian mechanics.

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner explains the particulate nature of matter, phase transitions, and latent heat, investigates cellular ultrastructure (osmosis, organelles, and plant vs. animal compartmentalization) using compound light microscopy, and calculates momentum, net force, and accelerations using Newton's Three Laws of Motion while verifying conservation of momentum experimentally."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **NCERT Grade 9 Science:** Ch 1 (*Matter in Our Surroundings*), Ch 5 (*The Fundamental Unit of Life*), Ch 9 (*Force and Laws of Motion*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Secondary Stage Science C-1.1 (Cellular organization and biological processes), C-2.1 (Chemical states and thermodynamics), C-3.1 (Newtonian mechanics and dynamics).
- **CBSE Secondary Science Learning Goals:** S9-01, S9-03, S9-06.

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: Kinetic Nature of Matter & Thermal Phase Changes:** Explain states of matter through intermolecular spaces and kinetic energy; differentiate boiling from evaporation; quantify latent heat of fusion and vaporization.
- **C2: Cell Ultrastructure & Membrane Transport:** Prepare temporary onion peel and cheek cell slides; examine cell organelles (nucleus, mitochondria, endoplasmic reticulum, plastids); demonstrate osmosis in hypertonic, hypotonic, and isotonic solutions.
- **C3: Newton's Laws of Motion & Momentum:** Apply Newton's First Law (inertia of rest, motion, direction), Second Law ($F = ma = \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$), and Third Law (action-reaction pairs); solve kinematic impulse problems.
- **C4: Conservation of Momentum & Collision Dynamics:** Formulate the law of conservation of momentum ($m_1 u_1 + m_2 u_2 = m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2$) and verify through balloon rocket tracks, marble collisions, or firearm recoil dynamics.

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: Kinetic Nature of Matter & Thermal Phase Changes

#### Task ID: `SCI-9-MATT-1.1`
- **Title:** Plot a heating curve for ice-to-steam to determine latent heat plateaus.
- **Time Estimate:** 40 min
- **Resource Level:** Medium (beaker with crushed ice, thermometer $-10^\circ\text{C}$ to $110^\circ\text{C}$, burner/spirit lamp, stopwatch)
- **Skill Type:** Experimental Thermodynamics & Data Logging
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Heating Curve Graph
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Records temperature every 1 minute while heating melting ice continuously.
  - Observes that temperature remains constant at $0^\circ\text{C}$ during melting and at $100^\circ\text{C}$ during boiling despite continuous heat input.
  - Explains latent heat: Thermal energy is absorbed to overcome intermolecular forces of attraction rather than increase kinetic energy.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Science, Chapter 1, pp. 6–9; NCF-SE Science C-2.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Temperature-time data recording grid with safety instructions for open flame.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Why does steam at $100^\circ\text{C}$ cause significantly more severe burns than boiling water at $100^\circ\text{C}$? Calculate latent heat of vaporization."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: गुप्त ऊष्मा (Gupt Ooshma - Latent Heat), संगलन की गुप्त ऊष्मा (Sangalan ki Gupt Ooshma - Latent Heat of Fusion), क्वथनांक (Kwathnaank - Boiling Point)
  - Tamil: மறைவெப்பம் (Maraiveppam)

#### Task ID: `SCI-9-MATT-1.2`
- **Title:** Measure cooling rates produced by surface evaporation across four solvents of varying volatility.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Medium (water, ethanol/hand sanitizer, acetone/nail polish remover, cooking oil, 4 cotton swabs, laboratory thermometer)
- **Skill Type:** Controlled Comparative Inquiry
- **Assessment Mode:** Data Table & Written Analysis
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Wraps thermometer bulb in cotton swab soaked in test liquid; records temperature drop over 3 minutes.
  - Ranks liquids by cooling effect: Acetone > Ethanol > Water > Cooking Oil.
  - Correlates evaporative cooling with intermolecular attraction strength and latent heat of vaporization.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Science, Chapter 1, pp. 9–11.
- **Scaffold Support:** Safety protocol for volatile liquids away from flames.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Explain how earthen pots (Matkas) cool water through microscopic capillary pores using evaporative thermodynamics."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: वाष्पीकरण (Vaashpeekaran - Evaporation), शीतलन प्रभाव (Sheetlan Prabhaav - Cooling Effect)

### Sub-Competency C3: Newton's Laws of Motion & Momentum

#### Task ID: `SCI-9-NEWTON-3.1`
- **Title:** Calculate acceleration and force from ticker-tape or photogate kinematic data ($F = ma$).
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Medium (pulley, dynamic cart/trolley, slotted weights, meter ruler, stopwatch / photogate)
- **Skill Type:** Quantitative Dynamics & Graphical Verification
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Problem Set & Lab Report
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Calculates acceleration $a = \frac{v - u}{t}$ for a cart accelerated by a hanging mass.
  - Varies mass on cart while keeping pulling force constant; proves $a \propto \frac{1}{m}$.
  - Varies hanging weight while keeping cart mass constant; proves $a \propto F$.
  - Synthesizes Newton's Second Law: $F = k \cdot ma$, setting $k = 1$ to define 1 Newton.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Science, Chapter 9, pp. 119–123; CBSE Class 9 Science.
- **Scaffold Support:** Formula breakdown sheet showing derivation of $F = \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta t} = \frac{m(v - u)}{t} = ma$.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Why does a cricket fielder pull his hands backward while catching a fast ball? Explain in terms of impulse $\Delta p = F \times \Delta t$."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: संवेग (Samveg - Momentum), त्वरण (Tvaran - Acceleration), बल (Bal - Force), जड़त्व (Jadatva - Inertia)
  - Kannada: ಆವೇಗ (Aavega - Momentum), ಬಲ (Bala - Force)

### Sub-Competency C4: Conservation of Momentum & Collision Dynamics

#### Task ID: `SCI-9-COLL-4.1`
- **Title:** Verify the Law of Conservation of Momentum using colliding marbles along a grooved ruler track.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (two 30cm grooved plastic rulers, set of identical glass marbles, digital weighing scale / stopwatch)
- **Skill Type:** Collision Dynamics & Momentum Verification
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Experimental Proof
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Lines up 4 stationary marbles touching in a groove.
  - Rolls 1 marble into the row; observes exactly 1 marble departs from the far end at identical speed.
  - Rolls 2 marbles touching together; observes exactly 2 marbles depart together.
  - Proves algebraically: Initial Momentum $\sum m_i u_i$ equals Final Momentum $\sum m_i v_i$ in the absence of external unbalanced forces.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Science, Chapter 9, pp. 124–128.
- **Scaffold Support:** Marble collision recording template with elastic collision vector diagrams.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Calculate the recoil velocity of a 4 kg rifle when firing a 50 g bullet at $400\text{ m/s}$."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: संवेग संरक्षण का नियम (Samveg Sanrakshan ka Niyam), प्रतिक्षेप वेग (Pratikshep Veg - Recoil Velocity)
  - Gujarati: વેગમાન સંરક્ષણનો નિયમ (Vegman Sanrakshanno Niyam)
