# Grade 7 Social Science: Medieval India, Trade Networks & Mughal Administration
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Social Science (Middle Stage, Grade 7 - Core Cross-Disciplinary)  
**Curriculum Documents:** NCERT Grade 7 Social Science (Our Pasts - II: Chapter 2 *Kings and Kingdoms*, Chapter 4 *The Mughal Empire*, Chapter 6 *Towns, Traders and Craftspersons*); NCF-SE 2023 Middle Stage Social Science Competencies C-1.1, C-1.2, C-2.1, C-3.1.  
**Focus:** Historical causation, economic connectivity, imperial administrative systems (Mansabdari, Zabt), coastal trade hubs, and cultural syncretism.

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner evaluates medieval Indian political and administrative systems (Chola village governance, the Mughal Mansabdari and Zabt land revenue frameworks), traces internal and maritime trade routes connecting temple towns, administrative capitals, and port emporiums (Surat, Hampi, Masulipatnam), and analyzes primary historical records (inscriptions, coins, travelers' accounts) to evaluate economic prosperity and social structure."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **NCERT Grade 7 Social Science:** Our Pasts - II Ch 2 (*Kings and Kingdoms*), Ch 4 (*The Mughal Empire*), Ch 6 (*Towns, Traders and Craftspersons*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Middle Stage Social Science C-1.1 (Source-based inquiry), C-2.1 (Spatial trade geography), C-3.1 (Administrative and agrarian institutions).
- **CBSE Learning Outcomes:** SS7-02, SS7-04, SS7-07.

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: Chola Agrarian Expansion & Village Assemblies:** Examine Chola irrigation engineering (canals, tanks, embankments) and decipher the Uttaramerur inscription detailing democratic election rules for Sabha committees.
- **C2: Mughal Imperial State & Mansabdari Framework:** Deconstruct the dual rank system of Zat (personal status/salary) and Sawar (cavalry troops to maintain); contrast Jagirs (revenue assignments) with cash salaries.
- **C3: Agrarian Revenue Systems (Zabt & Todar Mal's Bandobast):** Analyze how Raja Todar Mal surveyed crop yields, prices, and cultivated areas over a 10-year period (1570–1580) to fix cash tax rates.
- **C4: Urban Emporiums, Guilds & Financial Instruments:** Map maritime trade networks linking Surat, Masulipatnam, and Hampi to the Indian Ocean; explain the financial security of Hundis (bills of exchange) and merchant guilds (Manigramam, Nanadesi).

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: Chola Agrarian Expansion & Village Assemblies

#### Task ID: `SOC-7-MED-1.1`
- **Title:** Decipher election qualifications and disqualifications in the Chola Uttaramerur Inscription.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (translated excerpt of 10th-century Chola inscription from Tamil Nadu)
- **Skill Type:** Epigraphical Source Analysis & Democratic Heritage
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Source Analysis & Comparative Table
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Identifies qualification criteria for Sabha committee candidates: age 35–70, ownership of taxable land, knowledge of Vedas, administrative honesty.
  - Identifies strict disqualification rule: Anyone who has served on a committee for 3 consecutive years without submitting accounts cannot contest.
  - Explains the Kudavolai (pot lottery) method: Names written on palm leaves, placed in a clay pot, drawn by a young boy.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Our Pasts - II, Chapter 2, pp. 27–28; NCF-SE C-1.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Palm leaf lottery graphic organizer with side-by-side translation.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Compare the Chola Kudavolai system with modern Indian secret ballot elections under the Election Commission of India."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: उत्तरमेरूर अभिलेख (Uttaramerur Abhilekh), कुदावोलाई (Kudavolai - Pot Ballot), सभा (Sabha), उर (Ur)
  - Tamil: உத்தரமேரூர் கல்வெட்டு (Uttaramerur Kalvettu), குடவோலை முறை (Kudavolai Murai)

### Sub-Competency C2: Mughal Imperial State & Mansabdari Framework

#### Task ID: `SOC-7-MUGH-2.1`
- **Title:** Calculate military quotas and revenue balances for a 5000 Zat / 5000 Sawar Mughal Mansabdar.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet, calculation ledger sheet)
- **Skill Type:** Mathematical Modeling & Institutional Analysis
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Financial Ledger
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Explains meaning of Zat (numerical rank determining status and salary) and Sawar (number of cavalrymen required to maintain).
  - Calculates annual troop maintenance expenses: horses, branding (Dagh), armor, fodder, and trooper stipends.
  - Distinguishes between Naqd (cash payment) and Jagir (assignment of land revenue).
  - Analyzes why corruption occurred when Jagirdars extracted excessive taxes knowing they would be transferred soon.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Our Pasts - II, Chapter 4, pp. 51–53.
- **Scaffold Support:** Flowchart showing Mansabdar rank tier -> Troops inspected -> Jagir revenue collected.
- **Extension Challenge:** "How did the crisis in the Jagirdari system during the late 17th century contribute to peasant rebellions and the decline of the empire?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: मनसबदार (Mansabdar), जात (Zat), सवार (Sawar), जागीर (Jagir), दाग़ (Dagh - Horse Branding)
  - Urdu: منصب دار (Mansabdar), جاگیر (Jagir)

### Sub-Competency C3: Agrarian Revenue Systems (Zabt & Todar Mal's Bandobast)

#### Task ID: `SOC-7-MUGH-3.1`
- **Title:** Analyze Raja Todar Mal's 10-year land survey (Zabt) and create a tax assessment schedule.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet with mock historical crop yield data table)
- **Skill Type:** Economic History & Quantitative Analysis
- **Assessment Mode:** Data Table & Written Reflection
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Explains the 10-year Dahsala settlement (1570–1580): surveying crop yields, market prices, and cultivated areas.
  - Calculates the state share fixed at one-third ($\frac{1}{3}$) of average yield converted into cash.
  - Explains why Zabt could only be implemented in provinces where revenue officials could physically survey land (not in Bengal or Gujarat coastal swamps).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Our Pasts - II, Chapter 4, pp. 53–54; NCF-SE C-3.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Table template showing: Crop -> 10-Year Average Yield -> State 1/3 Share -> Cash Rate.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Why did peasant monetization (paying taxes in silver rupees rather than grain) force cultivators into debt with local moneylenders?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: ज़ब्त प्रणाली (Zabt Pranalee), राजा टोडर मल (Raja Todar Mal), दहसाला बंदोबस्त (Dahsaala Bandobast)
  - Marathi: महसूल पद्धत (Mahasul Paddhat)

### Sub-Competency C4: Urban Emporiums, Guilds & Financial Instruments

#### Task ID: `SOC-7-TRADE-4.1`
- **Title:** Trace the maritime route of a Gujarat merchant and issue a medieval Hundi (bill of exchange).
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (map of Indian Ocean trade networks, mock calligraphic Hundi parchment sheet)
- **Skill Type:** Economic Cartography & Financial Literacy
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Document Draft
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Traces shipping route from Surat port through Gulf of Cambay across the Arabian Sea to Basra and Mocha.
  - Drafts a mock Hundi (financial note promising payment at a distant city minus discount fee).
  - Explains why Surat Hundis were honored in distant overseas financial markets like Cairo, Antwerp, and Basra without moving heavy silver coins.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Our Pasts - II, Chapter 6, pp. 84–87; NCF-SE C-2.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Annotated model of an authentic 17th-century merchant Hundi.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Compare the medieval Hundi system with modern international banking instruments like Letters of Credit and SWIFT bank wire transfers."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: हुंडी (Hundi - Bill of Exchange), सूरत बंदरगाह (Surat Bandarbaah - Port), व्यापारिक संघ (Vyaapaarik Sangh - Guild)
  - Gujarati: હુંડી (Hundi), વેપારી મહાજન (Vepari Mahajan)
