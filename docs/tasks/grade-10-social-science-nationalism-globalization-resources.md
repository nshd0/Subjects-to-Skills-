# Grade 10 Social Science: Nationalism, Globalization & Resource Planning
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Social Science (Secondary Stage, Grade 10 - Board Examination Readiness)  
**Curriculum Documents:** NCERT Grade 10 India and the Contemporary World - II (Chapter 1 *The Rise of Nationalism in Europe*; Chapter 2 *Nationalism in India*); NCERT Grade 10 Contemporary India - II (Chapter 1 *Resources and Development*); NCERT Grade 10 Understanding Economic Development (Chapter 4 *Globalization and the Indian Economy*); NCF-SE 2023 Secondary Stage Social Science Competencies C-1.1, C-2.1, C-3.1, C-4.1.  
**Focus:** Board examination readiness: Source-based historical questions, map plotting, multi-factor economic analysis of liberalization and foreign trade, and resource sustainability plans.

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner evaluates the emergence of nation-states and anti-colonial movements in Europe and India (Satyagraha, Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, Salt March), assesses the multi-dimensional impacts of globalization and 1991 economic liberalisation on domestic producers and workers, and formulates sustainable resource planning strategies incorporating land conservation, soil classification, and ecological renewal (Agenda 21)."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **NCERT Grade 10 Social Science:** History Ch 1 & 2 (*Rise of Nationalism in Europe* & *Nationalism in India*); Geography Ch 1 (*Resources and Development*); Economics Ch 4 (*Globalization and the Indian Economy*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Secondary Stage Social Science C-1.1 (Historical anti-colonial nationalism & democratic movements), C-2.1 (Geographic resource conservation & spatial analysis), C-4.1 (Economic globalization, labor rights, and international trade governance).
- **CBSE Class 10 Board Assessment Blueprint:** SS10-01, SS10-04, SS10-06.

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: Anti-Colonial Mass Movements & Satyagraha in India:** Deconstruct Gandhi’s philosophy of Satyagraha (Champaran, Kheda, Ahmedabad); analyze the Non-Cooperation-Khilafat Movement (1920–22), Chauri Chaura withdrawal, the 1930 Salt March from Sabarmati to Dandi, and the limits of Civil Disobedience (Dalits, Poona Pact, minority communities).
- **C2: Visual Allegories & Nationalist Symbolism:** Interpret visual nationalism: Frédéric Sorrieu’s 1848 utopian prints in Europe, Marianne and Germania allegories, and the visual iconography of Bharat Mata (Abanindranath Tagore), tricolor flags (Swadeshi and Swaraj flags), and folklore revival.
- **C3: Economic Globalization & MNC Integration:** Trace how Multinational Corporations (MNCs) integrate global production chains (foreign direct investment, joint ventures, outsourcing); evaluate the 1991 New Economic Policy (liberalisation, removal of trade barriers); critique the World Trade Organization (WTO) and advocate for Fair Globalization.
- **C4: Resource Planning, Soil Geography & Sustainability:** Classify resources (Biotic/Abiotic, Renewable/Non-renewable, Individual/Community/National/International); identify land degradation causes (over-irrigation, overgrazing, mining); map India’s 6 soil zones (Alluvial, Black/Regur, Red & Yellow, Laterite, Arid, Forest); evaluate Rio Summit 1992 and Agenda 21.

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: Anti-Colonial Mass Movements & Satyagraha in India

#### Task ID: `SOC-10-HIST-1.1`
- **Title:** Analyze the strategic and symbolic significance of Mahatma Gandhi's 1930 Dandi Salt March.
- **Time Estimate:** 40 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (historical primary source letters, map of Gujarat coastline)
- **Skill Type:** Historical Source Analysis & Strategic Reasoning
- **Assessment Mode:** Board Exam 5-Mark Question Response
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Explains why salt was chosen as a universal rallying symbol: Consumed equally by rich and poor; British state salt monopoly and tax affected every single household.
  - Traces the 240-mile march from Sabarmati Ashram to coastal Dandi with 78 volunteers over 24 days.
  - Distinguishes Civil Disobedience (actively breaking colonial laws) from Non-Cooperation (refusing to collaborate with colonial institutions).
  - Evaluates nationwide response: Manufacturing illegal salt, boycotting foreign cloth, picketing liquor shops, non-payment of Chaukidari taxes.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 10 India and the Contemporary World - II, Chapter 2, pp. 38–41; CBSE Board History.
- **Scaffold Support:** Chronological timeline graphic organizer with cause-and-effect arrows.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Analyze the Poona Pact of September 1932 between Dr. B.R. Ambedkar and Mahatma Gandhi regarding separate electorates vs reserved seats."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: सविनय अवज्ञा आंदोलन (Savinay Avagya Aandolan - Civil Disobedience), दांडी मार्च (Dandi March), सत्याग्रह (Satyagraha)
  - Gujarati: દાંડી કૂચ (Dandi Kooch), સત્યાગ્રહ (Satyagraha)

### Sub-Competency C2: Visual Allegories & Nationalist Symbolism

#### Task ID: `SOC-10-HIST-2.1`
- **Title:** Compare the visual allegories of Bharat Mata, Marianne, and Germania in fostering national identity.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet with 3 historical paintings / lithographs)
- **Skill Type:** Iconographic & Art Historical Analysis
- **Assessment Mode:** Comparative Source Analysis
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Analyzes Abanindranath Tagore's 1905 painting of Bharat Mata: Depicted as an ascetic figure, calm, divine, dispensing food, cloth, and learning.
  - Analyzes Marianne of France (red Phrygian cap of liberty, cockade, tricolor) and Germania (crown of oak leaves symbolizing heroism).
  - Explains how personifying an abstract nation as a female figure forged emotional collective belonging across diverse language groups.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 10 India and the Contemporary World - II, Chapter 1, p. 23; Chapter 2, pp. 47–49.
- **Scaffold Support:** Feature-by-feature iconographic symbol breakdown chart.
- **Extension Challenge:** "How did the design of the Swaraj tricolor flag change from the 1906 Bengal Swadeshi flag (8 lotuses) to Gandhi's 1921 flag with the spinning wheel?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: भारत माता (Bharat Mata), रूपक (Roopak - Allegory), सामूहिक अपनेपन का भाव (Saamoohik Apnepaan ka Bhaav - Collective Belonging)
  - Bengali: ভারত মাতা (Bharat Mata), রূপক (Rupok)

### Sub-Competency C3: Economic Globalization & MNC Integration

#### Task ID: `SOC-10-ECON-3.1`
- **Title:** Trace the global production chain of a multinational smartphone and evaluate the impact of 1991 liberalization.
- **Time Estimate:** 40 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (case study dossier on smartphone manufacturing components)
- **Skill Type:** Economic Supply Chain & Policy Analysis
- **Assessment Mode:** Global Supply Chain Diagram & Policy Essay
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Maps global production splitting: Designed in USA $\rightarrow$ Microchips manufactured in Taiwan $\rightarrow$ Assembled in India/China $\rightarrow$ Customer support in India.
  - Explains how MNCs lower costs: Manufacturing in low-wage countries, sourcing specialized components globally.
  - Explains India's 1991 New Economic Policy: Dismantling the 'License Raj', removing import quotas and duties, encouraging Foreign Direct Investment (FDI).
  - Contrasts benefits for skilled urban consumers with acute challenges faced by small-scale domestic manufacturing units (batteries, plastics, toys).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 10 Understanding Economic Development, Chapter 4, pp. 56–64; NCF-SE C-4.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** World map supply chain flowchart template with component callouts.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Evaluate how the World Trade Organization (WTO) enforces rules on developing nations while wealthy nations continue agricultural subsidies."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: वैश्वीकरण (Vaishveekaran - Globalization), बहुराष्ट्रीय कंपनी (Bahuraashtriya Company - MNC), उदारीकरण (Udaareekaran - Liberalisation), विदेशी प्रत्यक्ष निवेश (FDI)
  - Tamil: உலகமயமாக்கல் (Ulagamayamaakkal)

### Sub-Competency C4: Resource Planning, Soil Geography & Sustainability

#### Task ID: `SOC-10-GEOG-4.1`
- **Title:** Map India's major soil zones and formulate a regional soil conservation strategy.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (outline political map of India, colored pencils, soil classification chart)
- **Skill Type:** Cartographic Spatial Analysis & Agrarian Planning
- **Assessment Mode:** Map Work & Soil Management Matrix
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Correctly colors and labels on an outline map of India:
    - Alluvial Soil: Northern plains and river deltas.
    - Black / Regur Soil: Deccan trap lava plateau (ideal for cotton).
    - Red and Yellow Soil: Eastern and southern Deccan plateau.
    - Laterite Soil: High-temperature, high-rainfall leaching zones of Western Ghats.
    - Arid Soil: Western Rajasthan desert.
  - Identifies causes of soil erosion (gully erosion, sheet erosion, wind denudation).
  - Recommends 3 proven conservation techniques: Contour plowing, strip cropping, and shelter belts.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 10 Contemporary India - II, Chapter 1, pp. 6–12; CBSE Board Map Syllabus.
- **Scaffold Support:** India soil distribution reference map with color-coded legend guide.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Evaluate the resolutions of the 1992 Rio de Janeiro Earth Summit and the failure to achieve Agenda 21 sustainable development goals."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: जलोढ़ मृदा (Jalodh Mrida - Alluvial Soil), काली/रेगुर मृदा (Kaali/Regur Mrida - Black Soil), मृदा अपरदन (Mrida Apardan - Soil Erosion), आश्रय पट्टिका (Aashray Pattika - Shelter Belts)
  - Marathi: काळी माती (Kaali Maati - Regur), गाळाची माती (Gaalaachi Maati)
