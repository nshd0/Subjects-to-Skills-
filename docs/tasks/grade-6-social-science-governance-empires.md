# Grade 6 Social Science: Local Governance & Early Indian Empires
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Social Science (Middle Stage, Grade 6 - Foundational Bridge)  
**Curriculum Documents:** NCERT Grade 6 Social Science (Our Pasts - I: Chapter 3 *In the Earliest Cities*, Chapter 7 *Ashoka, The Emperor Who Gave Up War*; Social and Political Life - I: Chapter 5 *Panchayati Raj*, Chapter 6 *Rural Administration*, Chapter 7 *Urban Administration*); NCF-SE 2023 Middle Stage Social Science Competencies C-1.1, C-1.2, C-2.1, C-4.1.  
**Focus:** Concrete-to-abstract transition through simulated Gram Sabhas, municipal budget allocation, deciphering Ashokan rock edicts, and comparing Harappan drainage to modern urban planning.

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner evaluates how democratic decisions are made at the grassroots level through Panchayati Raj and Urban Municipalities, simulates civic deliberation and public budgeting, analyzes the administrative structures and ethical governance of early Indian empires (Ashoka's Mauryan administration and Harappan urbanism), and explains the continuity between ancient public welfare and contemporary citizenship."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **NCERT Grade 6 Social Science:** History Ch 3 (*In the Earliest Cities*), Ch 7 (*Ashoka, The Emperor Who Gave Up War*); Civics Ch 5 (*Panchayati Raj*), Ch 7 (*Urban Administration*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Middle Stage Social Science C-1.1 (Historical inquiry through material culture & inscriptions), C-4.1 (Grassroots democratic institutions and participatory citizenship).
- **CBSE Social Science Learning Outcomes:** SS6-03 (Civic mechanisms), SS6-06 (Interpreting historical sources and maps).

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: Grassroots Rural Democracy (Panchayati Raj):** Explain the structure and relationship between Gram Sabha, Gram Panchayat, and Block/Zilla Parishad; simulate participatory decision-making on community welfare.
- **C2: Urban Civic Administration & Public Services:** Map the roles of Ward Councillors, Municipal Commissioners, and sanitary committees; trace tax revenue flow to street lighting, garbage management, and public health.
- **C3: Ancient Urban Planning & Harappan Civic Engineering:** Examine Harappan citadel planning, grid road layouts, covered brick drainage networks, and granary storage.
- **C4: Imperial Administration & Ashokan Dhamma:** Deconstruct Mauryan administrative hierarchy (tribute, taxation, spies, governors) and analyze Ashoka’s Rock Edicts on non-violence, religious harmony, and public welfare.

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: Grassroots Rural Democracy (Panchayati Raj)

#### Task ID: `SOC-6-GOV-1.1`
- **Title:** Roleplay a Gram Sabha meeting deliberating an urgent village water and road repair agenda.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (classroom benches arranged in circle, meeting agenda sheet)
- **Skill Type:** Civic Simulation, Oral Argumentation & Deliberation
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Peer Observation
- **Track Alignment:** Track A (Compulsory Core)
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Distinguishes who qualifies as a Gram Sabha member (all adults aged 18+ registered to vote in village).
  - Simulates Sarpanch, Ward Panch, Gram Panchayat Secretary, and village resident roles.
  - Passes a democratic resolution addressing handpump deepening and BPL list verification.
  - Explains the oversight role of Gram Sabha in preventing Panchayat funds misuse.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Social and Political Life - I, Chapter 5, pp. 43–47.
- **Scaffold Support:** Role cards with prompt questions: "Resident: Ask where last year's road repair funds went; Secretary: Record minutes."
- **Extension Challenge:** "How does the reservation of one-third seats for women in Panchayats transform rural policy priorities?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: ग्राम सभा (Gram Sabha), ग्राम पंचायत (Gram Panchayat), सरपंच (Sarpanch), पंचायती राज (Panchayati Raj)
  - Tamil: கிராம சபை (Grama Sabai)

#### Task ID: `SOC-6-GOV-1.2`
- **Title:** Draw a hierarchical flowchart comparing the 3 tiers of Panchayati Raj with judicial/district linkages.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (drawing paper, colored pens)
- **Skill Type:** Conceptual Structuring & Visual Organization
- **Assessment Mode:** Diagram & Written Explanation
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Illustrates 3 tiers: Gram Panchayat (Village) -> Panchayat Samiti / Janpad (Block) -> Zilla Parishad (District).
  - Annotates funding flow and coordination from state government downward.
  - Identifies role of District Collector / CEO in administrative oversight.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Social and Political Life - I, Chapter 5, p. 48.
- **Scaffold Support:** Blank 3-tiered pyramid template with arrow connectors.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Compare the functions of a Nyaya Panchayat (informal village court) with the formal Sub-Divisional Magistrate court."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: जिला परिषद (Zilla Parishad), पंचायत समिति (Panchayat Samiti)
  - Telugu: జిల్లా పరిషత్ (Zilla Parishad), మండల పరిషత్ (Mandal Parishad)

### Sub-Competency C2: Urban Civic Administration & Public Services

#### Task ID: `SOC-6-URBAN-2.1`
- **Title:** Map a neighborhood municipal ward and trace the resolution path of a clogged drain complaint.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet, local neighborhood sketch map)
- **Skill Type:** Civic Inquiry & Procedural Knowledge
- **Assessment Mode:** Flowchart & Written Response
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Differentiates the elected Ward Councillor from the appointed Municipal Commissioner / administrative staff.
  - Outlines the 4-step grievance path: Citizen reporting -> Ward Councillor endorsement -> Departmental sanitation engineer -> Action dispatch.
  - Explains why uncollected municipal solid waste creates public health emergencies (malaria, cholera).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Social and Political Life - I, Chapter 7, pp. 58–63.
- **Scaffold Support:** Case study prompt modeled after Yasmin Khala's story from NCERT Chapter 7.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Investigate the door-to-door waste segregation model (green vs blue bins) mandated by Solid Waste Management Rules 2016."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: नगर निगम (Nagar Nigam - Municipal Corporation), वार्ड पार्षद (Ward Paarshad - Councillor), आयुक्त (Aayukt - Commissioner)
  - Marathi: महानगरपालिका (Mahanagarpalika)

#### Task ID: `SOC-6-URBAN-2.2`
- **Title:** Formulate a balanced budget allocation for a ₹10 Lakh Ward Improvement Grant.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (budget allocation worksheet, calculator)
- **Skill Type:** Mathematical Reasoning & Civic Prioritization
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Group Defense
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Allocates ₹10,00,000 across 5 priorities: Street lights, park maintenance, drainage repair, primary health dispensary, community library.
  - Ensures no category receives zero funds while justifying trade-offs.
  - Explains where municipal revenues originate (property tax, water tax, commercial licenses, state grants).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Social and Political Life - I, Chapter 7, p. 61.
- **Scaffold Support:** Percentage guideline wheel (e.g., minimum 30% for sanitation and health).
- **Extension Challenge:** "If tax collection drops by 25% during a drought year, which budget lines should be protected and why?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: संपत्ति कर (Sampatti Kar - Property Tax), चुंगी (Chungi - Octroi/Toll), बजट आवंटन (Budget Aavantan)
  - Bengali: পৌরসভা (Pouroshova), কর (Kar)

### Sub-Competency C3: Ancient Urban Planning & Harappan Civic Engineering

#### Task ID: `SOC-6-HARAPPA-3.1`
- **Title:** Compare Harappan covered drainage systems with modern town drainage using cross-sectional diagrams.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (drawing sheet, ruler, colored pencils)
- **Skill Type:** Comparative Historical Analysis & Technical Drawing
- **Assessment Mode:** Illustrated Cross-Section & Written Response
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Draws Harappan street layout showing main drains along straight streets with gentle slopes.
  - Details covered drains with inspection holes and limestone/terracotta covers for regular cleaning.
  - Contrasts this with open sewage gutters observed in unplanned modern localities; writes a 4-sentence reflection on ancient sanitation foresight.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Our Pasts - I, Chapter 3, pp. 25–28; NCF-SE C-1.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Reference archaeological diagram of Mohenjo-daro drainage systems from NCERT.
- **Extension Challenge:** "How did Harappan standard burnt brick dimensions (ratio 4:2:1) enable massive interlocking structural strength across hundreds of settlements?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: हड़प्पा सभ्यता (Harappa Sabhyata), ढकी हुई नालियां (Dhaki hui Naaliyan), दुर्ग (Durg - Citadel)
  - Gujarati: હડપ્પીય સંસ્કૃતિ (Harappiya Sanskruti)

#### Task ID: `SOC-6-HARAPPA-3.2`
- **Title:** Analyze the architectural features and civic purpose of the Great Bath at Mohenjo-daro.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet with archaeological site photo/plan)
- **Skill Type:** Source Interpretation & Evidence-Based Deduction
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Peer Discussion
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Identifies 3 engineering features: fired bricks lined with plaster, waterproofing layer of natural bitumen/tar, steps leading down from north and south.
  - Notes adjacent rooms and brick-lined well for clean water inflow.
  - Explains archaeological hypothesis: A public tank intended for special ritual bathing rather than domestic bathing.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Our Pasts - I, Chapter 3, p. 25.
- **Scaffold Support:** Guided observation prompts: "What kept the water from leaking out through the bricks? Where did the water come from?"
- **Extension Challenge:** "Compare the Great Bath with stepwells (Baolis/Vavs) constructed in Gujarat and Rajasthan during medieval times."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: विशाल स्नानागार (Vishaal Snaanaagaar), प्राकृतिक कोलतार (Praakritik Koltaar - Natural Bitumen)
  - Kannada: ಮಹಾ ಸ್ನಾನಗೃಹ (Maha Snanagruha)

### Sub-Competency C4: Imperial Administration & Ashokan Dhamma

#### Task ID: `SOC-6-ASHOKA-4.1`
- **Title:** Transcribe and analyze Major Rock Edict XIII (Kalinga War transformation and Dhamma).
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (reproduction excerpt of Brahmi script and English/Hindi translation)
- **Skill Type:** Historical Primary Source Analysis & Moral Reflection
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Source Analysis
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Reads excerpt describing King Devanampiya Piyadassi's deep sorrow over Kalinga casualties (100,000 killed, 150,000 deported).
  - Explains the profound historical shift from Bherighosha (war drums/conquest by force) to Dhammaghosha (conquest by righteousness/morality).
  - Identifies Ashoka's core Dhamma tenets: obedience to parents, respect for teachers, kindness to servants/slaves, and non-injury to animals.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Our Pasts - I, Chapter 7, pp. 67–70.
- **Scaffold Support:** Side-by-side glossary matching ancient words (Piyadassi, Dhamma, Bherighosha) to modern equivalents.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Why did Ashoka carve edicts in Prakrit, Greek, and Aramaic using regional scripts rather than exclusive court Sanskrit?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: धम्म (Dhamma), कलिंग युद्ध (Kalinga Yuddh), शिलालेख (Shilaalekh - Rock Inscription), ब्राह्मी लिपि (Brahmi Lipi)
  - Odia: କଳିଙ୍ଗ ଯୁଦ୍ଧ (Kalinga Juddha), ଅଶୋକଙ୍କ ଧର୍ମ (Ashokanka Dharma)

#### Task ID: `SOC-6-ASHOKA-4.2`
- **Title:** Map Mauryan administrative centers and compare taxation with tribute.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (outline map of India, colored pencils)
- **Skill Type:** Cartographic Mapping & Economic Distinction
- **Assessment Mode:** Map Work & Written Table
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Plots capital Pataliputra, Taxila (north-west trade gateway), and Ujjain (central sea-route gateway).
  - Differentiates taxes (regular, compulsory collections assessed on crops/trade) from tribute (irregular gifts brought voluntarily or coerced from forest tribes: timber, elephants, honey).
  - Explains how spies (Gudhapurushas) kept Emperor informed about provincial governors.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 6 Our Pasts - I, Chapter 7, pp. 65–67.
- **Scaffold Support:** Legend symbols for capital, provincial centers, and resource tribute areas.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Compare Kautilya's Arthashastra view of statecraft with Ashoka's welfare inscriptions on road wells and herbal medicine gardens."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: कर (Kar - Tax), नजराना/भेंट (Nazrana/Bhent - Tribute), पाटलिपुत्र (Pataliputra), गुप्तचर (Guptchar - Spies)
  - Tamil: வரி (Vari), திறை (Thirai)
