# Multidisciplinary Project Blueprint (Grade 7)
## "School & Community Water Audit: Conservation, Flow Rates & Chemical Testing"

**Target Grade Band:** Middle Stage (Grade 7, Ages 12–13 - Core Cross-Disciplinary)  
**Curricular Integration:** Science (Heat, Acids/Bases & Kinematics) + Mathematics (Fractions & Simple Equations) + AI (Pattern Recognition & Rule Systems) + Social Science (Chola Irrigation & Medieval Waterworks)  
**Duration:** 3–4 Weeks (Integrated across 12 class periods)  
**Real-World Context:** Indian educational institutions and municipalities face widespread water wastage due to leaking fixtures, inefficient distribution, and unmonitored chemical quality (acidity, hardness, microbial contamination).

---

## 1. Project Challenge Statement

> *"How can we, as student water conservators and investigative scientists, measure volumetric tap flow rates, test water pH acidity using natural indicators, model school water consumption using algebraic equations, design an automated leak-detection decision matrix, and publish an evidence-based 'Water Quality & Conservation Audit' modeled on historical Chola tank systems?"*

## 3. Project Phase Breakdown & Workflow

```
[ Phase 1: Physical Audit & Flow Rate Timing ] (Week 1)
  ├── Time water discharge into 1-liter marked containers (SCI-7-TIME-1.1)
  ├── Calculate fractional flow rates in L/min across all 20 school taps (MATH-7-FRAC-2.2)
  └── Research medieval Chola tank maintenance committees (SOC-7-MED-1.1)

[ Phase 2: Chemical Testing & Mathematical Modeling ] (Week 2)
  ├── Test tap water, borewell water, and RO water using turmeric & hibiscus (SCI-7-ACID-3.1)
  ├── Write linear balance equations for overhead tank depletion (MATH-7-EQN-3.1)
  └── Measure open sump thermal evaporation rates (SCI-7-HEAT-2.1)

[ Phase 3: AI Rule Automation & Civic Presentation ] (Week 3-4)
  ├── Discover peak hour usage patterns across school schedule (AI-7-PATT-1.1)
  ├── Construct IF-THEN diagnostic rules for prompt pipe maintenance (AI-7-RULE-2.1)
  └── Present final "Campus Water Audit & Action Plan" to School Management Committee
```

---

## 4. Final Cumulative Deliverables

1. **Campus Water Flow & Quality Dashboard:**
   - A visual wall chart or interactive digital sheet displaying flow rate benchmarks, leaky tap flags, and chemical acidity ratings (safe neutral vs acidic/alkaline hazard).
2. **Mathematical Tank Capacity Model:**
   - Algebraic formulation calculating the exact hour the school overhead tank empties on high-usage sports days vs normal days, with recommendations for flow restrictors.
3. **Plumbing Maintenance Expert System Card:**
   - An easy-to-use visual laminated guide for school custodial staff using IF-THEN diagnostic rules to classify leaks (Minor Drip, Valve Gasket Failure, Pipe Burst) and required action.
