# Grade 10 Mathematics: Quadratic Equations, Triangles & Probability
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Mathematics (Secondary Stage, Grade 10 - Board Examination Readiness)  
**Curriculum Documents:** NCERT Grade 10 Mathematics (Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations; Chapter 6: Triangles; Chapter 15: Probability); NCF-SE 2023 Secondary Stage Mathematics Competencies C-1.1, C-2.1, C-4.1.  
**Focus:** Board examination readiness with competency-based question styles (Assertion-Reason, Case-Based Item Sets, Objective MCQs, and High Order Thinking Skills [HOTS]).

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner derives roots and analyzes the nature of roots for quadratic equations using factorisation and the quadratic formula ($b^2 - 4ac$), proves and applies geometric similarity theorems (Basic Proportionality Theorem / Thales' Theorem, criteria for similarity of triangles) to solve complex architectural and spatial problems, and calculates theoretical and experimental probabilities across single and compound events to evaluate risk in real-world scenarios."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **NCERT Grade 10 Mathematics:** Ch 4 (*Quadratic Equations*), Ch 6 (*Triangles*), Ch 15 (*Probability*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Secondary Stage Math C-1.1 (Algebraic roots & discriminant analysis), C-2.1 (Deductive geometric proofs & similarity), C-4.1 (Probability and stochastic modeling).
- **CBSE Board Examination Standards:** M10-02 (Quadratic applications), M10-05 (Geometry proofs), M10-11 (Probability and statistics).

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: Quadratic Roots & Discriminant Analysis:** Solve $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ using middle-term splitting and the quadratic formula ($x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$); analyze discriminant $D = b^2 - 4ac$ for distinct real roots ($D > 0$), equal real roots ($D = 0$), and non-real roots ($D < 0$).
- **C2: Real-World Quadratic Modeling (Board Case Studies):** Formulate quadratic equations from speed-distance (upstream/downstream river currents), geometric area boundaries, and industrial pipe filling rates.
- **C3: Geometric Similarity & Deductive Proofs:** Prove the Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales' Theorem) and its converse; apply AAA, SSS, and SAS similarity criteria to prove proportional relationships in complex figures.
- **C4: Classical & Experimental Probability:** Calculate theoretical probability $P(E) = \frac{\text{Number of outcomes favorable to } E}{\text{Total number of possible outcomes}}$; evaluate complementary events ($P(E) + P(\bar{E}) = 1$), sure and impossible events, and board-style card, dice, and defective product quality control problems.

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: Quadratic Roots & Discriminant Analysis

#### Task ID: `MATH-10-QUAD-1.1`
- **Title:** Derive the Quadratic Formula by completing the square and classify nature of roots using the discriminant.
- **Time Estimate:** 40 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet, board exam exemplar bank)
- **Skill Type:** Algebraic Derivation & Rigorous Proof
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Proof & Discriminant Table
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Carries out step-by-step derivation: $ax^2 + bx + c = 0 \rightarrow x^2 + \frac{b}{a}x = -\frac{c}{a} \rightarrow \left(x + \frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 = \frac{b^2 - 4ac}{4a^2} \rightarrow x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$.
  - Computes discriminant $D = b^2 - 4ac$ for 4 sample equations.
  - Classifies roots: Two distinct real roots ($D > 0$), two equal real roots ($D = 0$), no real roots ($D < 0$).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 10 Mathematics, Chapter 4, pp. 79–88; CBSE Class 10 Math Blueprint.
- **Scaffold Support:** Completing-the-square algebraic tiles visual organizer.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Find the values of $k$ for which the equation $(k - 12)x^2 + 2(k - 12)x + 2 = 0$ has equal real roots."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: द्विघात समीकरण (Dwigyhaat Sameekaran), विविक्तकर (Viviktakar - Discriminant), मूलों की प्रकृति (Moolon ki Prakriti)
  - Tamil: இருபடிச் சமன்பாடுகள் (Irubadi Samanpaadugal)

### Sub-Competency C2: Real-World Quadratic Modeling (Board Case Studies)

#### Task ID: `MATH-10-QUAD-2.1`
- **Title:** Model motorboat upstream and downstream river current speeds into a quadratic equation.
- **Time Estimate:** 40 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (CBSE Board 4-mark case study problem sheet)
- **Skill Type:** Word Problem Translation & Equation Solving
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Examination Item
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Sets up speed variables: Still water speed $v = 18\text{ km/h}$, stream speed $x\text{ km/h}$.
  - Expresses upstream speed as $18 - x$ and downstream speed as $18 + x$.
  - Writes time difference equation: $\frac{24}{18 - x} - \frac{24}{18 + x} = 1\text{ hour}$.
  - Clears fractions to form quadratic: $x^2 + 48x - 324 = 0$, factors to find $x = 6\text{ km/h}$, and discards negative root.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 10 Mathematics, Chapter 4, p. 87; CBSE Board 2019/2023 Exam Paper.
- **Scaffold Support:** Two-way river velocity diagram (against stream vs with stream).
- **Extension Challenge:** "Solve a two-water-tap tank filling problem where the larger tap takes 10 hours less than the smaller tap to fill the tank."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: धारा के प्रतिकूल (Dhaara ke Pratikool - Upstream), धारा के अनुकूल (Dhaara ke Anukool - Downstream)
  - Telugu: ప్రవాహానికి ఎదురుగా (Pravaahaaniki Edurugaa)

### Sub-Competency C3: Geometric Similarity & Deductive Proofs

#### Task ID: `MATH-10-TRI-3.1`
- **Title:** Prove the Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales' Theorem) using area ratio proofs.
- **Time Estimate:** 40 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (geometry sheet, ruler, compass)
- **Skill Type:** Deductive Theorem Proof
- **Assessment Mode:** Formal Board Proof Presentation
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Draws triangle $ABC$ with line $DE \parallel BC$ intersecting $AB$ at $D$ and $AC$ at $E$.
  - Writes formal Statement, Given, To Prove, Construction (join $BE, CD$; draw perpendiculars $DM \perp AC$ and $EN \perp AB$).
  - Proves: $\frac{\text{Area}(\Delta ADE)}{\text{Area}(\Delta BDE)} = \frac{AD}{DB}$ and $\frac{\text{Area}(\Delta ADE)}{\text{Area}(\Delta CDE)} = \frac{AE}{EC}$.
  - Cites equal area of triangles on same base between same parallels to establish $\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC}$.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 10 Mathematics, Chapter 6, Theorem 6.1, pp. 124–126; CBSE Standard Theorem.
- **Scaffold Support:** Color-coded proof outline highlighting shared triangle altitudes.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Prove the converse of Thales' Theorem using proof by contradiction."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: आधारभूत आनुपातिकता प्रमेय (Aadhaarboot Aanupaatikta Pramey - BPT / Thales Theorem), समरूप त्रिभुज (Samroop Tribhuj)
  - Kannada: ಮೂಲ ಅನುಪಾತ ಪ್ರಮೇಯ (Moola Anupaatha Prameya)

### Sub-Competency C4: Classical & Experimental Probability

#### Task ID: `MATH-10-PROB-4.1`
- **Title:** Evaluate theoretical probabilities and complementary events across a 52-card standard deck.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (standard 52-card playing deck or card probability worksheet)
- **Skill Type:** Combinatorial Probability & Risk Analysis
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Examination Item & Self-Check
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Categorizes 52 cards into suits (Spades, Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs) and face cards (12 kings, queens, jacks).
  - Computes probability of drawing: A red face card ($\frac{6}{52} = \frac{3}{26}$), a black king ($\frac{2}{52} = \frac{1}{26}$), not a spade ($1 - \frac{13}{52} = \frac{3}{4}$).
  - Verifies that sum of probabilities of all elementary events equals 1: $\sum P(e_i) = 1$.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 10 Mathematics, Chapter 15, pp. 296–308.
- **Scaffold Support:** Card deck visual matrix poster (13 ranks x 4 suits).
- **Extension Challenge:** "A bag contains 24 marbles, of which $x$ are green. If 6 more green marbles are put into the bag, the probability of drawing green doubles. Find $x$."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: प्रायिकता (Praayikta - Probability), पूरक घटना (Poorak Ghatna - Complementary Event), ताश के पत्ते (Taash ke Patte)
  - Bengali: সম্ভাবনা (Shombhabona), তাস (Taas)
