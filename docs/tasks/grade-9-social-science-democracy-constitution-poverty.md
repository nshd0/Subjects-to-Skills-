# Grade 9 Social Science: Democracy, Constitutional Design & Poverty as a Challenge
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Social Science (Secondary Stage, Grade 9 - Foundation Depth)  
**Curriculum Documents:** NCERT Grade 9 Democratic Politics - I (Chapter 1 *What is Democracy? Why Democracy?*; Chapter 2 *Constitutional Design*); NCERT Grade 9 Economics (Chapter 3 *Poverty as a Challenge*); NCF-SE 2023 Secondary Stage Social Science Competencies C-1.1, C-2.1, C-4.1.  
**Focus:** Political institutional design, constitutional philosophy, socio-economic deprivation metrics (poverty lines, caloric thresholds), and policy evaluation.

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner evaluates democratic decision-making against authoritarian alternatives, analyzes the philosophical values, institutional compromises, and drafting history of the Indian Constitution through Constituent Assembly Debates, calculates poverty thresholds using multidimensional indicators and caloric requirements, and critiques national anti-poverty policies (MGNREGA, PDS) in advancing socio-economic justice."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **NCERT Grade 9 Democratic Politics - I:** Ch 1 (*What is Democracy? Why Democracy?*), Ch 2 (*Constitutional Design*).
- **NCERT Grade 9 Economics:** Ch 3 (*Poverty as a Challenge*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Secondary Stage Social Science C-1.1 (Constitutional philosophy and political institutions), C-4.1 (Economic equity and development challenges).
- **CBSE Secondary Assessment Standards:** SS9-01, SS9-03, SS9-07.

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: Democratic Governance & Non-Democratic Counterpoints:** Dissect 4 essential democratic features: elected rulers make final decisions, free and fair electoral competition, one person one vote one value, rule of law and citizen rights; evaluate historical case studies (Pakistan under Musharraf, Zimbabwe under Mugabe).
- **C2: Constitutional Philosophy & Drafting Debates:** Trace the Constituent Assembly's deliberative process (Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, Jawaharlal Nehru's Objectives Resolution); dissect Preamble values (Sovereignty, Socialism, Secularism, Democracy, Republic, Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity).
- **C3: Poverty Metrics & Vulnerability Analysis:** Deconstruct how the poverty line is estimated in India using minimum nutritional standards (2400 kcal/day rural vs 2100 kcal/day urban) and consumer expenditure; analyze inter-state disparities and social vulnerability (SC/ST households, female-headed households).
- **C4: Anti-Poverty Programmes & Rights-Based Welfare:** Critique target mechanisms and leakages in national welfare schemes: Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA 2005), Public Distribution System (PDS), and Targeted Calorie Delivery.

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: Democratic Governance & Non-Democratic Counterpoints

#### Task ID: `SOC-9-DEM-1.1`
- **Title:** Evaluate non-democratic governance claims using the Four Core Democratic Feature Benchmarks.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet with 4 global case study excerpts)
- **Skill Type:** Comparative Political Analysis
- **Assessment Mode:** Matrix Table & Written Justification
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Evaluates 4 political systems: Pakistan under 2002 Legal Framework Order (military council veto), China's National People's Congress (only Communist Party candidates approved), Fiji's indigenous weighted voting, and Zimbabwe's ZANU-PF media censorship.
  - Matches each case study to the specific democratic feature it violates.
  - Explains why superficial elections without opposition freedom fail the democratic standard.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Democratic Politics - I, Chapter 1, pp. 4–10; NCF-SE C-1.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Decision matrix with 4 benchmark columns pre-filled.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Evaluate the argument: 'Democracy leads to instability and delays, whereas an efficient dictatorship develops faster.' Refute using historical famine and development data (Amartya Sen)."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: लोकतंत्र (Loktantra - Democracy), एक व्यक्ति एक वोट (Ek Vyakti Ek Vote), विधि का शासन (Vidhi ka Shaasan - Rule of Law)
  - Tamil: மக்களாட்சி (Makkalaatchi)

### Sub-Competency C2: Constitutional Philosophy & Drafting Debates

#### Task ID: `SOC-9-CONST-2.1`
- **Title:** Analyze a Constituent Assembly Debate excerpt on the balance between political and economic democracy.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (printed excerpt of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar's final speech to the Constituent Assembly on 25 November 1949)
- **Skill Type:** Primary Source Textual Criticism
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Source Analysis
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Interprets Dr. Ambedkar's warning: "On the 26th of January 1950, we are going to enter into a life of contradictions. In politics we will have equality, and in social and economic life we will have inequality."
  - Explains how political equality (one person, one vote) remains incomplete without socio-economic equality.
  - Identifies Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV) as the constitutional mechanism designed to guide socio-economic transformation.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Democratic Politics - I, Chapter 2, pp. 28–30; Constituent Assembly Debates (Vol. XI).
- **Scaffold Support:** Historical vocabulary glossary with margin explanatory callouts.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Compare the Indian Preamble with the South African Constitution's founding commitments to truth and reconciliation."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: संविधान सभा (Samvidhaan Sabha), प्रस्तावना (Prastaavna - Preamble), सामाजिक न्याय (Saamaajik Nyaay - Social Justice)
  - Bengali: গণপরিষদ (Gonoporishod), প্রস্তাবনা (Prostabona)

### Sub-Competency C3: Poverty Metrics & Vulnerability Analysis

#### Task ID: `SOC-9-POV-3.1`
- **Title:** Calculate rural vs urban monthly poverty lines from minimum nutritional intake standards.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet with Tendulkar and Rangarajan committee caloric benchmarks)
- **Skill Type:** Quantitative Economic Analysis
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Calculation Table
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Explains why rural caloric benchmark (2400 kcal/person/day) is higher than urban benchmark (2100 kcal/person/day) due to heavier manual agricultural labor.
  - Analyzes why rural monetary poverty line is lower than urban monetary poverty line despite higher calorie needs (due to higher urban prices for shelter, transport, and utilities).
  - Calculates daily household food budgets and evaluates whether ₹32/day is adequate for dignified human survival.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Economics, Chapter 3, pp. 31–34; NCF-SE C-4.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Caloric calculation chart showing food grain grams to kilocalorie conversion.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Examine the Multi-Dimensional Poverty Index (MPI) and explain why measuring health, schooling, and sanitation is superior to income-only poverty lines."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: निर्धनता रेखा (Nirdhanta Rekha - Poverty Line), कैलोरी आवश्यकता (Calorie Aavashyakta), बहुआयामी निर्धनता (Bahu-aayaami Nirdhanta - MPI)
  - Telugu: దారిద్య్రరేఖ (Daaridrya Rekha)

### Sub-Competency C4: Anti-Poverty Programmes & Rights-Based Welfare

#### Task ID: `SOC-9-POV-4.1`
- **Title:** Audit the implementation and legal guarantees of the MGNREGA 2005 Act in a mock rural panchayat.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (MGNREGA case study dossier, job card ledger worksheet)
- **Skill Type:** Policy Evaluation & Rights-Based Citizenship
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Audit Report
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Outlines the 4 legal statutory rights under MGNREGA 2005: 100 days guaranteed wage employment, work within 5 km of residence, unemployment allowance if work not given within 15 days, 33% jobs reserved for women.
  - Audits a village muster roll: Detects ghost workers, delayed wage disbursements, and inadequate water/creche facilities at worksites.
  - Calculates wage shortfall compensation owed to workers under the statutory timeline.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Economics, Chapter 3, pp. 38–40.
- **Scaffold Support:** Sample MGNREGA Job Card and muster roll verification guide.
- **Extension Challenge:** "How do MGNREGA works contribute to long-term climate resilience through water conservation and afforestation?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: मनरेगा (MGNREGA), रोजगार गारंटी (Rojgaar Guarantee), मस्टर रोल (Muster Roll)
  - Malayalam: തൊഴിലുറപ്പ് പദ്ധതി (Thozhilurappu Padhathi)
