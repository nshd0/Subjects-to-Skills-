# Grade 8 Mathematics: Linear Equations in One Variable
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Mathematics (Middle Stage, Grade 8)  
**Curriculum Document:** NCERT Grade 8 Mathematics, Chapter 2 (*Linear Equations in One Variable*); NCF-SE 2023 Curricular Goal C-2.1 & C-2.2 (Mathematical Thinking & Algebraic Generalization)  
**Competency Statement:** *"Formulate, solve, and verify algebraic linear equations in one variable with rational coefficients and apply them to model and solve real-world problems involving numbers, geometry, ages, and rates."*  
**Track Alignment:** Track A (Compulsory Core Curriculum)  
**Target Grade Band:** Middle Stage (Grade 8, Ages 13–14)  
**Multilingual Key Terms:**  
- **Linear Equation:** Hindi: एक चर वाले रैखिक समीकरण (*Ek Char Wale Raikhik Sameekaran*); Marathi: एका चलातील रेषीय समीकरणे (*Eka Chalateel Reshiya Sameekarane*); Kannada: ಒಂದು ಚರಾಕ್ಷರವಿರುವ ರೇಖಾತ್ಮಕ ಸಮೀಕರಣಗಳು (*Ondu Charaaksharaviruv Rekhaatmaka Sameekaranagalu*); Tamil: ஒரு மாறியில் அமைந்த நேரிய சமன்பாடுகள் (*Oru Maariyil Amaindha Neriya Samanpaadugal*)  
- **Variable:** Hindi: चर (*Char*); Marathi: चल (*Chal*); Kannada: ಚರಾಕ್ಷರ (*Charaakshara*); Tamil: மாறி (*Maari*)  
- **Constant:** Hindi: अचर (*Achar*); Marathi: स्थिर (*Sthir*); Kannada: ಸ್ಥಿರಾಂಕ (*Sthiraanka*); Tamil: மாறிலி (*Maarili*)  
- **Transposition:** Hindi: पक्षांतरण (*Pakshaantaran*); Marathi: पक्षांतर (*Pakshaantar*); Kannada: ಪಕ್ಷಾಂತರ (*Pakshaantara*); Tamil: இடமாற்றம் (*Idamaatram*)  

---

## Sub-Competency Architecture

- **Sub-Competency 1 (MATH-8-LINEQ-C1):** Translate verbal mathematical statements, real-world constraints, and number puzzles into formal algebraic linear equations.
- **Sub-Competency 2 (MATH-8-LINEQ-C2):** Solve linear equations having the variable on one side and rational numbers on the other using balance methods and inverse transposition.
- **Sub-Competency 3 (MATH-8-LINEQ-C3):** Solve complex linear equations having variables on both sides, parentheses, and fractional denominators.
- **Sub-Competency 4 (MATH-8-LINEQ-C4):** Apply linear equation modeling to solve authentic word problems involving geometry (perimeters/angles), consecutive numbers, age differences, and speed-time-distance.

## Detailed Task Catalog

### Sub-Competency 1: Algebraic Translation & Formulation (MATH-8-LINEQ-C1)

#### Task ID: MATH-8-LINEQ-1.1
- **Title:** Decode 6 verbal mathematical sentences into algebraic equations.
- **Time Estimate:** 20 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (Notebook, pencil)
- **Skill Type:** Conceptual Understanding & Mathematical Communication
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Worksheet
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Task Description:** Read 6 verbal statements (e.g., "The sum of three times a number and 11 is 32", "If you subtract 5 from 6 times a number, you get 7", "One-fourth of m is 3 more than 7") and write the corresponding algebraic equations with explicitly defined variable symbols.
- **Success Criteria:**
  1. Identifies the unknown entity and assigns a single letter variable (e.g., $x, y, m$).
  2. Correctly translates relational words ("sum", "exceeds", "times", "yields") into operators ($+, -, \times, =$).
  3. All 6 written equations are algebraically accurate.
- **Scaffold Support:** Provide a reference conversion table matching everyday words to mathematical symbols (e.g., "increased by" $\rightarrow +$, "is" $\rightarrow =$).
- **Extension Challenge:** Write 2 reverse verbal statements for given equations: $4x - 7 = 15$ and $\frac{y}{3} + 2 = 10$.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Class 8 Mathematics, Ch. 2, Section 2.1, pp. 21–22; NCF-SE 2023 Middle Stage Math Benchmark 2.1.
- **Reviewer Notes:** Essential diagnostic task to verify algebraic fluency before procedural manipulation.

#### Task ID: MATH-8-LINEQ-1.2
- **Title:** Model classroom daily budgets and school canteen tallies as linear equations.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (Price list handout, paper)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural & Contextual Modeling
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Problem Set
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Task Description:** Given a school canteen price list (Samosa: ₹12, Juice: ₹20), students model 4 budgeting scenarios: (1) Ananya buys 3 samosas and some juices with ₹100 note and gets ₹4 change; (2) Rahul has ₹150 and wants to buy twice as many notebooks as pens. Formulate equations for each.
- **Success Criteria:**
  1. Models total expenditure as $(Unit\ Price \times Quantity) + Change = Total$.
  2. Expresses multi-variable relationships in terms of a single variable ($2x$ and $x$).
- **Scaffold Support:** Pre-structured fill-in-the-blank equation template: `(Price per item * x) + fixed cost = total amount`.
- **Extension Challenge:** Formulate an equation where a discount voucher of ₹25 applies to total purchases exceeding ₹200.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Class 8 Mathematics, Ch. 2, Example 5, p. 25.
- **Reviewer Notes:** Low resource requirement; relatable everyday context enhances engagement.

#### Task ID: MATH-8-LINEQ-1.3
- **Title:** Deconstruct number riddles into algebraic equations.
- **Time Estimate:** 20 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (Blackboard / Peer Pair Cards)
- **Skill Type:** Critical Thinking & Logical Deduction
- **Assessment Mode:** Peer Review / Oral Explanation
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Task Description:** In pairs, solve 3 classic Indian mathematical riddles (e.g., "Think of a number. Double it, add 18, divide by 2, subtract original number. What remains?"). Express the steps algebraically to prove why the answer is always constant.
- **Success Criteria:**
  1. Expresses step-by-step arithmetic as $\frac{2x + 18}{2} - x$.
  2. Simplifies algebraic expression to show invariant constant result ($9$).
- **Scaffold Support:** Walk through the arithmetic with a concrete number (e.g., $x = 5$) before switching to variable $x$.
- **Extension Challenge:** Invent a personalized number riddle that always results in the student's birth month.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Class 8 Mathematics, Ch. 2, Exercise 2.2 preamble, p. 28.
- **Reviewer Notes:** Fosters joy in mathematical patterns and demystifies algebra.

### Sub-Competency 2: Solving Equations with Variable on One Side (MATH-8-LINEQ-C2)

#### Task ID: MATH-8-LINEQ-2.1
- **Title:** Solve 5 linear equations using the balance scale method and verify by substitution.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (Worksheet, graph paper or balance drawing)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural Skill & Verification
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Worksheet
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Task Description:** Solve 5 single-variable linear equations (e.g., $2x - 3 = 7$; $\frac{x}{3} + \frac{5}{2} = -\frac{3}{2}$; $14y - 8 = 13$). For each equation, illustrate the balance scale action (adding/subtracting identical quantities to both LHS and RHS) and formally check the solution by LHS=RHS substitution.
- **Success Criteria:**
  1. Accurately determines the unknown variable value.
  2. Demonstrates the inverse operation on both sides of the equality sign.
  3. Completes explicit verification showing $LHS = RHS$.
- **Scaffold Support:** Color code operations: green for addition inverses, red for multiplication/division inverses.
- **Extension Challenge:** Solve an equation containing fractional coefficients with unlike denominators: $\frac{2x}{3} + 1 = \frac{7x}{15} + 3$.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Class 8 Mathematics, Ch. 2, Section 2.2, Examples 1–4, pp. 22–24.
- **Reviewer Notes:** Emphasizes verification habit, reducing algebraic calculation slips.

#### Task ID: MATH-8-LINEQ-2.2
- **Title:** Execute transposition drills to isolate variables in 4 steps or fewer.
- **Time Estimate:** 20 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (Printed flashcards or whiteboard)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural Fluency
- **Assessment Mode:** Timed Self-Check / Quiz
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Task Description:** Practice the rapid transposition rule (shifting a term from LHS to RHS changes its sign: $+ \leftrightarrow -, \times \leftrightarrow \div$) across 6 progressive equations: (1) $x - 2 = 7$, (2) $y + 3 = 10$, (3) $6 = z + 2$, (4) $\frac{3}{7} + x = \frac{17}{7}$, (5) $6x = 12$, (6) $\frac{t}{5} = 10$.
- **Success Criteria:**
  1. Solves all 6 equations with 100% correct sign transposition.
  2. Identifies that terms transferred across the equality sign invert their operations.
- **Scaffold Support:** Transposition rule reminder card kept on desk.
- **Extension Challenge:** Explain why multiplying both sides by zero is an invalid algebraic transposition step.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Class 8 Mathematics, Ch. 2, Exercise 2.1, p. 23.
- **Reviewer Notes:** Low resource requirement; ideal for diagnostic bell-ringer activity.

### Sub-Competency 3: Equations with Variables on Both Sides & Parentheses (MATH-8-LINEQ-C3)

#### Task ID: MATH-8-LINEQ-3.1
- **Title:** Solve equations with variables on both sides by grouping like terms.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (Worksheet)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural & Analytical Reasoning
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Task Description:** Solve 4 multi-term equations having variable terms on both LHS and RHS: (1) $2x - 3 = x + 2$, (2) $5t - 3 = 3t - 5$, (3) $5x + 9 = 5 + 3x$, (4) $8x + 4 = 3(x - 1) + 7$. Clearly demonstrate grouping variable terms to LHS and constants to RHS.
- **Success Criteria:**
  1. Collects like terms on the same side with accurate sign adjustments.
  2. Correctly expands parentheses using the distributive property before transposing.
  3. Verifies final root by substitution into both sides.
- **Scaffold Support:** Provide highlighters to mark variable terms in yellow and constant numbers in blue.
- **Extension Challenge:** Solve: $m - \frac{m-1}{2} = 1 - \frac{m-2}{3}$ and state the LCM used for clearance.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Class 8 Mathematics, Ch. 2, Section 2.3, pp. 28–30.
- **Reviewer Notes:** Common error hotspot: sign error during distribution of negative multiplier outside brackets.

#### Task ID: MATH-8-LINEQ-3.2
- **Title:** Simplify equations reducible to linear form using Cross-Multiplication.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (Worksheet)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural Fluency & Fractional Manipulation
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Problem Set
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Task Description:** Solve equations in the form $\frac{ax+b}{cx+d} = \frac{p}{q}$ (e.g., $\frac{x+1}{2x+3} = \frac{3}{8}$ and $\frac{9x}{7 - 6x} = 15$) using the cross-multiplication technique. Write out the formal justification for why multiplying crosswise preserves equality.
- **Success Criteria:**
  1. Applies cross-multiplication: $q(ax+b) = p(cx+d)$.
  2. Correctly isolates $x$ and checks for non-zero denominator condition.
- **Scaffold Support:** Step-by-step diagonal cross arrow visual aid.
- **Extension Challenge:** Determine the value of $k$ for which $\frac{2x+5}{3x+k} = 1$ has no solution or an undefined denominator when $x = -2$.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Class 8 Mathematics, Ch. 2, Section 2.5 ("Equations Reducible to the Linear Form"), pp. 33–35.
- **Reviewer Notes:** Builds foundation for Grade 9-10 rational algebraic fractions.

### Sub-Competency 4: Real-World Applications & Word Problem Modeling (MATH-8-LINEQ-C4)

#### Task ID: MATH-8-LINEQ-4.1
- **Title:** Calculate geometric dimensions of a rectangular school playground.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (Ruler, worksheet, pencil)
- **Skill Type:** Applied Problem Solving & Geometric Synthesis
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Problem Solution
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Task Description:** The perimeter of a rectangular school playground is 154 meters. Its length is 2 meters more than twice its breadth. Students: (1) Define breadth as variable $x$, (2) Express length in terms of $x$, (3) Formulate perimeter equation $2(Length + Breadth) = 154$, (4) Solve for $x$ and find both dimensions, (5) Calculate area of playground.
- **Success Criteria:**
  1. Sets up the correct equation: $2(2x + 2 + x) = 154$.
  2. Finds breadth $= 25\text{ m}$ and length $= 52\text{ m}$.
  3. Accurately verifies perimeter $= 2(52 + 25) = 154\text{ m}$ and computes area $= 1300\text{ m}^2$.
- **Scaffold Support:** Draw a rectangle on the board and label breadth $= x$ and length $= 2x + 2$.
- **Extension Challenge:** If fencing costs ₹45 per meter and turfing costs ₹80 per square meter, calculate total school expenditure.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Class 8 Mathematics, Ch. 2, Exercise 2.2, Question 2, p. 28.
- **Reviewer Notes:** Interdisciplinary link to school campus planning and architectural estimation.

#### Task ID: MATH-8-LINEQ-4.2
- **Title:** Solve multi-generational age disparity problems.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (Worksheet)
- **Skill Type:** Logical Reasoning & Algebraic Formulation
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Peer Verification
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Task Description:** Baichung's father is 26 years younger than Baichung's grandfather and 29 years older than Baichung. The sum of the ages of all three is 135 years. Students define Baichung's age as $x$, formulate the linear equation, solve for all three individuals' ages, and verify the total.
- **Success Criteria:**
  1. Writes correct expressions: Father $= x + 29$, Grandfather $= x + 29 + 26$.
  2. Sets up and solves: $x + (x + 29) + (x + 55) = 135 \Rightarrow 3x + 84 = 135 \Rightarrow x = 17$.
  3. States ages: Baichung (17), Father (46), Grandfather (72).
- **Scaffold Support:** Timeline graphic organizer showing age differences relative to a reference person.
- **Extension Challenge:** Formulate an equation to determine in how many years the grandfather's age will be exactly triple Baichung's age.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Class 8 Mathematics, Ch. 2, Exercise 2.2, Question 11, p. 29.
- **Reviewer Notes:** Popular board exam question type that tests translation of multi-relational word statements.

#### Task ID: MATH-8-LINEQ-4.3
- **Title:** Model vehicle speed, travel time, and rendezvous distance.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (Worksheet, map diagram)
- **Skill Type:** Mathematical Modeling & Real-World Synthesis
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Worksheet
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Task Description:** Two motorbikes start simultaneously from school and town (60 km apart) towards each other. One travels 5 km/h faster than the other. If after 2 hours they are 10 km apart, formulate the linear equation and determine both speeds.
- **Success Criteria:**
  1. Applies $Distance = Speed \times Time$ to both vehicles: $2s + 2(s + 5) + 10 = 60$.
  2. Solves for $s = 10\text{ km/h}$ and $s + 5 = 15\text{ km/h}$.
  3. Verifies distance sum equals initial separation.
- **Scaffold Support:** Number line track showing starting points at 0 and 60 with arrow segments representing distance covered in 2 hours.
- **Extension Challenge:** If the faster bike starts 30 minutes after the first bike, formulate the revised time-offset equation.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Class 8 Mathematics, Ch. 2, Exercise 2.4, pp. 31–32; NCF-SE 2023 Curricular Goal C-2.4.
- **Reviewer Notes:** Direct interdisciplinary link to Science (Motion) and Safe School Zone project.
