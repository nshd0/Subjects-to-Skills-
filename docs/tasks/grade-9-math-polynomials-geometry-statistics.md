# Grade 9 Mathematics: Polynomials, Coordinate Geometry & Statistics
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Mathematics (Secondary Stage, Grade 9 - Foundation Depth)  
**Curriculum Documents:** NCERT Grade 9 Mathematics (Chapter 2: Polynomials; Chapter 3: Coordinate Geometry; Chapter 14: Statistics); NCF-SE 2023 Secondary Stage Mathematics Competencies C-1.1, C-2.1, C-4.1.  
**Focus:** Secondary stage algebraic depth, Cartesian plane navigation, empirical data distributions, and alignment with CBSE board-preparatory reasoning.

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner factors polynomials and applies remainder and factor theorems to algebraic functions, navigates and plots geometric loci on the 2D Cartesian coordinate plane across four quadrants, and collects, groups, and graphically represents statistical data using histograms and frequency polygons while calculating measures of central tendency (mean, median, mode) to evaluate real-world claims."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **NCERT Grade 9 Mathematics:** Ch 2 (*Polynomials*), Ch 3 (*Coordinate Geometry*), Ch 14 (*Statistics*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Secondary Stage Math C-1.1 (Algebraic structures and factorization), C-2.1 (Cartesian coordinate geometry), C-4.1 (Statistical distributions and central measures).
- **CBSE Secondary Assessment Standards:** M9-02, M9-03, M9-08.

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: Polynomial Operations & Algebraic Identities:** Classify polynomials by degree and terms, evaluate zeros, apply Remainder and Factor Theorems, and expand 3-variable and cubic identities ($(x+y+z)^2$, $(x+y)^3$, $x^3+y^3+z^3-3xyz$).
- **C2: Factorization of Quadratic & Cubic Polynomials:** Factor quadratic trinomials via middle-term splitting and decompose cubic polynomials by combining rational root testing with polynomial long division.
- **C3: The Cartesian Coordinate Plane & Spatial Plotting:** Plot ordered pairs $(x, y)$ across all 4 quadrants and axes, calculate collinearity, and model geometric figures on coordinate grids.
- **C4: Grouped Statistics & Measures of Central Tendency:** Construct frequency distribution tables with uniform class intervals, draw histograms with varying widths, plot frequency polygons, and compute mean, median, and mode for grouped and ungrouped datasets.

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: Polynomial Operations & Algebraic Identities

#### Task ID: `MATH-9-POLY-1.1`
- **Title:** Verify zeros of linear and quadratic polynomials algebraically and geometrically.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet, graph paper)
- **Skill Type:** Conceptual Verification & Graphical Thinking
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Plot
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Evaluates $p(x)$ at candidate values to test if $p(k) = 0$.
  - Plots $y = 2x - 4$ and $y = x^2 - 4$ on graph paper.
  - Explains why the geometric zero is the exact X-intercept where the curve crosses the X-axis ($y = 0$).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Mathematics, Chapter 2, pp. 31–35; CBSE Class 9 Math Blueprint.
- **Scaffold Support:** Table of values generator: $x \rightarrow p(x) \rightarrow (x, y)$ coordinate point.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Can a polynomial of degree $n$ have more than $n$ real zeros? Prove using the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: बहुपद के शून्यक (Bahupad ke Shoonyak - Zeros of Polynomial), घात (Ghaat - Degree)
  - Tamil: பல்லுறுப்புக் கோவையின் பூஜ்ஜியங்கள் (Palluruppuk Kovaiyin Poojiyangal)

#### Task ID: `MATH-9-POLY-1.2`
- **Title:** Apply the Remainder Theorem to find remainders without performing long division.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet)
- **Skill Type:** Procedural Efficiency & Theorem Application
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Self-Check
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - States theorem: If $p(x)$ is divided by $(x - a)$, the remainder equals $p(a)$.
  - Finds remainder for $p(x) = x^4 + x^3 - 2x^2 + x + 1$ divided by $(x - 1)$ by evaluating $p(1)$.
  - Verifies result using traditional polynomial long division for 1 problem.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Mathematics, Chapter 2, pp. 35–40.
- **Scaffold Support:** Step-by-step checklist: Set divisor $(x - a) = 0 \rightarrow x = a \rightarrow$ substitute $a$ into $p(x)$.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Find the value of $k$ if $2x - 1$ is a factor of $4x^3 + 2x^2 - 8x + k$."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: शेषफल प्रमेय (Sheshphal Pramey - Remainder Theorem), भाज्य (Bhaajya), भाजक (Bhaajak)
  - Kannada: ಶೇಷ ಪ್ರಮೇಯ (Shesha Prameya)

### Sub-Competency C2: Factorization of Quadratic & Cubic Polynomials

#### Task ID: `MATH-9-FACT-2.1`
- **Title:** Factor quadratic trinomials $ax^2 + bx + c$ by splitting the middle term.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet, algebra tiles)
- **Skill Type:** Algebraic Manipulation & Factorization
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Peer Review
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Identifies product $ac$ and sum $b$.
  - Finds integer pair $(p, q)$ such that $p \times q = ac$ and $p + q = b$.
  - Factors polynomials with leading coefficients $> 1$ (e.g., $6x^2 + 17x + 5 = (2x + 5)(3x + 1)$).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Mathematics, Chapter 2, pp. 40–44.
- **Scaffold Support:** Factor sum-product cross grid graphic organizer.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Factor $12x^2 - 7x + 1$ using geometric area decomposition tiles."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: मध्य पद विभक्तिकरण (Madhya Pad Vibhaktikaran), गुणनखंड (Gunankhand - Factors)
  - Telugu: వర్గ సమీకరణాల కారకాలు (Varga Sameekaranaala Kaarakaalu)

#### Task ID: `MATH-9-FACT-2.2`
- **Title:** Decompose a cubic polynomial $p(x) = x^3 - 23x^2 + 142x - 120$ using Factor Theorem and synthetic division.
- **Time Estimate:** 40 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet)
- **Skill Type:** Multi-Step Analytical Reasoning
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Response & Board Practice
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Tests constant term factors ($\pm 1, \pm 2, \pm 3 \dots$) to discover first zero $x = 1$.
  - Divides $p(x)$ by $(x - 1)$ to obtain quadratic quotient $x^2 - 22x + 120$.
  - Factors quadratic quotient to obtain all three linear factors: $(x - 1)(x - 10)(x - 12)$.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Mathematics, Chapter 2, pp. 43–45.
- **Scaffold Support:** Flowchart: Test trial root -> Divide by $(x - a)$ -> Factor quadratic quotient.
- **Extension Challenge:** "If $x + y + z = 0$, prove that $x^3 + y^3 + z^3 = 3xyz$ and use it to evaluate $(-12)^3 + 7^3 + 5^3$ without cubing."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: त्रिघात बहुपद (Trighaat Bahupad - Cubic Polynomial), गुणनखंड प्रमेय (Gunankhand Pramey)
  - Bengali: ত্রিঘাত বহুপদী (Trighat Bohupodi)

### Sub-Competency C3: The Cartesian Coordinate Plane & Spatial Plotting

#### Task ID: `MATH-9-COORD-3.1`
- **Title:** Plot vertices of geometric polygons across all four quadrants and compute bounded perimeter.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (graph paper, 30cm ruler, pencil)
- **Skill Type:** Spatial Visualization & Cartesian Coordinate Plotting
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Coordinate Graph
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Identifies origin $(0, 0)$, abscissa ($x$-coordinate), and ordinate ($y$-coordinate).
  - Plots points in Quadrants I $(+,+)$, II $(-,+)$, III $(-,-)$, and IV $(+,-)$.
  - Plots $A(-3, 2), B(5, 2), C(5, -4), D(-3, -4)$; joins points to form rectangle and calculates perimeter.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Mathematics, Chapter 3, pp. 58–66; NCF-SE Math C-2.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** 4-quadrant sign convention chart prominently displayed.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Find the coordinates of the reflection of point $P(-4, 7)$ in (a) the X-axis, (b) the Y-axis, (c) the origin."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: कार्तीय तल (Karteeya Tal - Cartesian Plane), भुज (Bhuj - Abscissa), कोटि (Koti - Ordinate), चतुर्थांश (Chaturthaansh - Quadrant)
  - Marathi: निर्देशक भूमिती (Nirdeshak Bhumiti)

### Sub-Competency C4: Grouped Statistics & Measures of Central Tendency

#### Task ID: `MATH-9-STAT-4.1`
- **Title:** Construct a grouped frequency distribution table and histogram for class exam scores.
- **Time Estimate:** 40 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (dataset of 40 raw scores, graph paper, ruler)
- **Skill Type:** Data Grouping & Graphical Construction
- **Assessment Mode:** Graph Sheet & Group Evaluation
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Calculates range ($\text{Maximum} - \text{Minimum}$) and determines class interval size (e.g., 0–10, 10–20).
  - Uses continuous class boundaries and accurate tally marks.
  - Draws histogram with adjacent rectangles (zero gaps between bars) and a kink/zigzag mark if X-axis does not start at 0.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Mathematics, Chapter 14, pp. 245–252.
- **Scaffold Support:** Pre-printed tally table with class limits pre-marked.
- **Extension Challenge:** "How do you construct a histogram when class widths are unequal? Calculate adjusted frequency densities."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: आयतचित्र (Aayatchitra - Histogram), वर्ग अंतराल (Varg Antaraal - Class Interval), बारंबारता (Baarambaarta - Frequency)
  - Gujarati: સ્તંભાલેખ (Stambhaalekh)

#### Task ID: `MATH-9-STAT-4.2`
- **Title:** Calculate and compare Mean, Median, and Mode for an income distribution with extreme outliers.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet, calculator)
- **Skill Type:** Critical Statistical Analysis & Measure Comparison
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Analysis & Report
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Computes arithmetic mean: $\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x_i}{n}$.
  - Computes median by ordering values and finding $\frac{n+1}{2}$-th or average of middle terms.
  - Explains why the median is resistant to extreme millionaire outliers while the mean is heavily skewed upward.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 9 Mathematics, Chapter 14, pp. 259–265.
- **Scaffold Support:** Median formula reference card for odd vs even sample sizes.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Demonstrate the empirical relationship: $\text{Mode} \approx 3\text{Median} - 2\text{Mean}$ on moderately skewed data."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: माध्य (Maadhya - Mean), माध्यक (Maadhyak - Median), बहुलक (Bahulak - Mode)
  - Tamil: கூட்டுச் சராசரி (Koottu Charasari), இடைநிலை (Idainilai), முகடு (Mugadu)
