# Grade 7 Science: Motion & Time, Heat, and Acids, Bases & Salts
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Science (Middle Stage, Grade 7 - Core Cross-Disciplinary)  
**Curriculum Documents:** NCERT Grade 7 Science (Chapter 4: Heat; Chapter 5: Acids, Bases and Salts; Chapter 13: Motion and Time); NCF-SE 2023 Middle Stage Science Competencies C-1.2, C-2.1, C-3.1, C-3.2.  
**Focus:** Laboratory measurement, graphical modeling of kinematics, thermal energy transfer mechanisms, and kitchen chemistry indicator titration.

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner calculates speed and interprets distance-time graphs using experimental data from simple pendulums and rolling objects, investigates thermal energy transfer (conduction, convection, radiation) using laboratory and clinical thermometers, and identifies acidic, basic, and neutral substances using natural indicators (litmus, turmeric, China rose) while demonstrating neutralization reactions."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **NCERT Grade 7 Science:** Ch 4 (*Heat*), Ch 5 (*Acids, Bases and Salts*), Ch 13 (*Motion and Time*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Middle Stage Science C-2.1 (Quantitative measurement and timekeeping), C-3.1 (Thermodynamics and heat transfer), C-3.2 (Chemical changes and indicator testing).
- **CBSE Learning Objectives:** S7-03, S7-05, S7-08.

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: Kinematics & Periodic Timekeeping:** Measure time period of a simple pendulum, verify invariance with amplitude/mass, calculate speed ($\text{Distance} / \text{Time}$), and plot distance-time graphs.
- **C2: Thermal Energy Transfer & Measurement:** Measure temperatures using clinical and laboratory thermometers; demonstrate conduction in metals, convection currents in water/air, and radiant heat absorption.
- **C3: Acids, Bases & Natural Indicators:** Prepare natural indicator solutions (turmeric paste, red cabbage/China rose petals, litmus); classify domestic liquids by color transitions.
- **C4: Neutralization Reactions & Practical Applications:** Execute acid-base neutralization reactions; observe temperature changes (exothermic reaction); explain indigestion antacids, wasp/ant sting relief, and soil pH treatment.

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: Kinematics & Periodic Timekeeping

#### Task ID: `SCI-7-TIME-1.1`
- **Title:** Measure the time period of a simple pendulum across varying string lengths.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (cotton thread, metallic nut/pebble, stopwatch / smartphone timer)
- **Skill Type:** Precision Timing & Empirical Measurement
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Data Table
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Suspends a bob with string lengths of 60 cm, 80 cm, and 100 cm.
  - Times 20 full oscillations and computes time period $T = \frac{\text{Total Time}}{20}$.
  - Concludes that time period increases with length but is independent of bob mass and small displacement angles.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Science, Chapter 13, pp. 145–148.
- **Scaffold Support:** Oscillation counting guide: "Start count when bob passes central mean position in one direction."
- **Extension Challenge:** "How did Galileo use his pulse to observe the isochronism of swinging chandeliers in the cathedral of Pisa?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: सरल लोलक (Saral Lolak - Simple Pendulum), आवर्तकाल (Aavartkaal - Time Period), दोलन (Dolan - Oscillation)
  - Tamil: எளிய ஊசல் (Eliya Oosal), அலைவு நேரம் (Alaivu Neram)

#### Task ID: `SCI-7-TIME-1.2`
- **Title:** Plot and interpret distance-time graphs for uniform vs non-uniform motion of a toy car.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (stopwatch, measuring tape, toy car / rolling marble, graph paper)
- **Skill Type:** Graphical Representation & Kinematic Analysis
- **Assessment Mode:** Graph & Written Interpretation
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Records distance covered at 2-second intervals along a hallway floor.
  - Plots time on X-axis and distance on Y-axis with appropriate scaling.
  - Interprets graph slope: straight diagonal line = uniform speed; horizontal flat line = stationary state; curved line = non-uniform accelerated speed.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Science, Chapter 13, pp. 151–154; NCF-SE Science C-2.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Pre-scaled graph template with labeled axis intervals.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Calculate the vehicle speed in m/s and convert to km/h from the gradient $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}$ of your graph."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: दूरी-समय ग्राफ (Doori-Samay Graph), एकसमान गति (Eksamaan Gati - Uniform Motion)
  - Telugu: దూర-కాల గ్రాఫ్ (Doora-Kaala Graph)

### Sub-Competency C2: Thermal Energy Transfer & Measurement

#### Task ID: `SCI-7-HEAT-2.1`
- **Title:** Compare laboratory and clinical thermometers and measure cooling rates of warm water.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Medium (clinical thermometer, laboratory thermometer $-10^\circ\text{C}$ to $110^\circ\text{C}$, beaker/cup of warm water)
- **Skill Type:** Experimental Measurement & Instrument Comparison
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Comparative Table
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Identifies kink (constriction) in clinical thermometer preventing mercury backflow; explains why laboratory thermometer lacks kink.
  - Reads liquid meniscus at eye level without tilting.
  - Logs temperature every 2 minutes for 10 minutes to plot water cooling curve.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Science, Chapter 4, pp. 36–40.
- **Scaffold Support:** Thermometer reading safety card: Never hold by the bulb, wash before reading.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Why can clinical thermometers not be used to measure boiling water? Explain range limits ($35^\circ\text{C} - 42^\circ\text{C}$)."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: डॉक्टरी थर्मामीटर (Doctori Thermometer), प्रयोगशाला तापमापी (Prayogshaala Taapmaapi), पारा (Paara - Mercury)
  - Bengali: থার্মোমিটার (Thermometer), তাপমাত্রা (Taapmaatra)

#### Task ID: `SCI-7-HEAT-2.2`
- **Title:** Demonstrate heat conduction along an aluminum/iron rod using wax-fixed paper clips.
- **Time Estimate:** 25 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (metal ruler/wire, candle wax, 4 paper clips, candle/spirit lamp, clamp)
- **Skill Type:** Concrete Demonstration & Heat Flow Tracking
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Observation & Scientific Explanation
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Fixes 4 paper clips along metal rod at equal 3 cm intervals using melted candle wax.
  - Heats one end of rod with flame; observes clips falling sequentially from closest to furthest.
  - Concludes heat transfers through solids from hotter to colder end via particle vibration (conduction).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Science, Chapter 4, pp. 40–42.
- **Scaffold Support:** Teacher-supervised laboratory safety boundary setup.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Repeat experiment with a wooden dowel or glass rod. Explain the atomic difference between thermal conductors and insulators."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: चालन (Chaalan - Conduction), सुचालक (Suchaalak - Conductor), कुचालक (Kuchaalak - Insulator)
  - Marathi: वहन (Vahan), सुवाहक (Suvaahak)

### Sub-Competency C3: Acids, Bases & Natural Indicators

#### Task ID: `SCI-7-ACID-3.1`
- **Title:** Prepare turmeric and China rose indicators and test 6 common kitchen substances.
- **Time Estimate:** 35 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (turmeric powder, warm water, China rose / hibiscus petals, lemon juice, soap solution, vinegar, baking soda, curd)
- **Skill Type:** Biochemical Preparation & Indicator Testing
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Multi-Color Matrix
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Makes turmeric paper strips (yellow) and extracts hibiscus petal solution (light pink).
  - Tests 6 solutions; observes turmeric turns reddish-brown in basic solutions (soap, baking soda) and remains yellow in acidic/neutral.
  - Observes China rose turns magenta/dark pink in acids and green in bases.
  - Tabulates substances correctly as acidic, basic, or neutral.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Science, Chapter 5, pp. 50–53; NCF-SE Science C-3.2.
- **Scaffold Support:** Pre-printed color-transition prediction chart.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Why does a yellow curry stain on a white shirt turn reddish-brown when washed with soap, and turn back yellow when rinsed with lemon juice?"
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: अम्ल (Amla - Acid), क्षारक (Kshaarak - Base), सूचक (Soochak - Indicator), हल्दी सूचक (Haldi Soochak)
  - Gujarati: એસિડ (Acid), બેઝ (Base), સૂચક (Soochak)

### Sub-Competency C4: Neutralization Reactions & Practical Applications

#### Task ID: `SCI-7-ACID-4.1`
- **Title:** Perform an acid-base neutralization reaction with indicator and verify temperature rise.
- **Time Estimate:** 30 min
- **Resource Level:** Medium (dilute hydrochloric acid / vinegar, sodium hydroxide / baking soda solution, phenolphthalein / turmeric, test tube, thermometer)
- **Skill Type:** Chemical Reaction & Thermochemical Observation
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & Laboratory Write-up
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Adds indicator to base; slowly titrates acid drop-by-drop until color completely changes (neutral point).
  - Touches base of test tube or measures temperature before and after reaction to confirm exothermic temperature increase.
  - Writes word equation: $\text{Acid} + \text{Base} \rightarrow \text{Salt} + \text{Water} + \text{Heat}$.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 7 Science, Chapter 5, pp. 54–55.
- **Scaffold Support:** Pipette drop-counting worksheet with step-by-step color threshold checkpoints.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Explain why farmers add slaked lime ($\text{Ca(OH)}_2$) to acidic soil and organic compost to basic soil."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: उदासीनीकरण (Udaaseeneekaran - Neutralization), लवण (Lavan - Salt), ऊष्माक्षेपी (Ooshmakshepee - Exothermic)
  - Kannada: ತಟಸ್ಥೀಕರಣ (Tatashtheekarana)
