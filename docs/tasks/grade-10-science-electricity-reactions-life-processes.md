# Grade 10 Science: Electricity, Chemical Reactions & Life Processes
## Task-Based Learning (TBL) Competency Decomposition

**Curricular Subject:** Science (Secondary Stage, Grade 10 - Board Examination Readiness)  
**Curriculum Documents:** NCERT Grade 10 Science (Chapter 1: Chemical Reactions and Equations; Chapter 6: Life Processes; Chapter 12: Electricity); NCF-SE 2023 Secondary Stage Science Competencies C-1.1, C-2.1, C-3.1.  
**Focus:** Board examination readiness: Balancing reaction stoichiometry, verifying Ohm's Law and circuit resistor combinations, and deconstructing human organ systems with high-yield diagrammatic and Assertion-Reason practice.

---

## 1. Competency Statement

> *"The learner balances chemical equations and classifies reaction types (redox, precipitation, decomposition) with stoichiometric accuracy, calculates equivalent resistance, current, voltage, and electrical heating power across complex series-parallel circuits using Ohm's and Joule's Laws, and traces physiological mechanisms across human nutrition, double circulation, nephron ultrafiltration, and stomatal gas exchange."*

### Key Curricular References & Standards:
- **NCERT Grade 10 Science:** Ch 1 (*Chemical Reactions and Equations*), Ch 6 (*Life Processes*), Ch 12 (*Electricity*).
- **NCF-SE 2023:** Secondary Stage Science C-1.1 (Biochemical systems and metabolic transport), C-2.1 (Chemical transformation and stoichiometry), C-3.1 (Electromagnetism and circuit physics).
- **CBSE Secondary Assessment Standards:** S10-01, S10-04, S10-07.

---

## 2. Sub-Competencies Breakdown

- **C1: Chemical Equations & Reaction Classifications:** Balance complex chemical equations using atom conservation; classify combination, thermal/photolytic/electrolytic decomposition, single displacement, double displacement (precipitation), and redox reactions ($\text{oxidizing}$ vs $\text{reducing}$ agents).
- **C2: Human Metabolic Systems & Life Processes:** Detail autotrophic photosynthesis (light/dark reactions) and human holozoic nutrition; compare aerobic vs anaerobic glycolysis; trace systemic and pulmonary double circulation in the 4-chambered heart; detail nephron ultrafiltration in the kidneys.
- **C3: Electric Current, Potential Difference & Ohm's Law:** Measure current ($I$) with ammeters and potential difference ($V$) with voltmeters; verify Ohm's Law graphically ($V \propto I$); calculate resistivity $\rho = \frac{RA}{L}$ across wire materials.
- **C4: Series-Parallel Resistance & Joule's Heating Effect:** Derive equivalent resistance formulas ($R_s = R_1 + R_2 + \dots$ and $\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \dots$); calculate electrical energy and power ($P = VI = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$); apply Joule's Law of Heating ($H = I^2 Rt$) to domestic fuses and appliances.

## 3. Discrete Task Decomposition

### Sub-Competency C1: Chemical Equations & Reaction Classifications

#### Task ID: `SCI-10-CHEM-1.1`
- **Title:** Balance complex chemical equations and identify oxidizing and reducing agents in redox reactions.
- **Time Estimate:** 40 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (worksheet with 10 chemical reaction word problems)
- **Skill Type:** Stoichiometric Balancing & Redox Analysis
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Examination Item & Self-Check
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Balances equations using atom count tables: e.g., $\text{Fe} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4 + \text{H}_2$ ($3\text{Fe} + 4\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4 + 4\text{H}_2$).
  - Analyzes redox reaction: $\text{MnO}_2 + 4\text{HCl} \rightarrow \text{MnCl}_2 + 2\text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{Cl}_2$.
  - Identifies substance oxidized ($\text{HCl} \rightarrow \text{Cl}_2$), substance reduced ($\text{MnO}_2 \rightarrow \text{MnCl}_2$), oxidizing agent ($\text{MnO}_2$), and reducing agent ($\text{HCl}$).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 10 Science, Chapter 1, pp. 11–13; CBSE Board Question Bank.
- **Scaffold Support:** Reactant vs product atom balancing inventory worksheet.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Explain rancidity and corrosion (rusting of iron) as everyday undesirable oxidation phenomena and propose 3 chemical inhibitors."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: रासायनिक समीकरण का संतुलन (Rasayanik Sameekaran ka Santulan), उपचयन और अपचयन (Upchayan aur Apchayan - Oxidation and Reduction)
  - Tamil: வேதியியல் சமன்பாடுகள் (Vedhiyal Samanpaadugal)

### Sub-Competency C2: Human Metabolic Systems & Life Processes

#### Task ID: `SCI-10-BIO-2.1`
- **Title:** Trace blood flow through the four-chambered human heart during one complete double circulation cycle.
- **Time Estimate:** 40 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (anatomical diagram sheet of human heart and circulatory loop, colored pens)
- **Skill Type:** Anatomical Tracing & Physiological System Analysis
- **Assessment Mode:** Illustrated Flowchart & Written Explanation
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Traces deoxygenated blood from vena cava $\rightarrow$ right atrium $\rightarrow$ right ventricle $\rightarrow$ pulmonary artery $\rightarrow$ lungs.
  - Traces oxygenated blood from pulmonary veins $\rightarrow$ left atrium $\rightarrow$ left ventricle $\rightarrow$ aorta $\rightarrow$ systemic body capillaries.
  - Explains the evolutionary significance of complete chamber separation in mammals: prevents mixing of oxygenated and deoxygenated blood to maintain high constant body temperature.
  - Distinguishes pulmonary circulation from systemic circulation.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 10 Science, Chapter 6, pp. 105–108; NCF-SE Science C-1.1.
- **Scaffold Support:** Color-coding guide: Blue ink for deoxygenated blood, Red ink for oxygenated blood.
- **Extension Challenge:** "Why are ventricle muscular walls significantly thicker than atrium walls? Explain arterial vs venous blood pressure."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: दोहरा परिसंचरण (Dohra Parisancharan - Double Circulation), आलिंद (Aalind - Atrium), निलय (Nilay - Ventricle), महाधमनी (Mahaadhamanee - Aorta)
  - Marathi: दुहेरी रक्ताभिसरण (Duheri Raktabhisaran)

### Sub-Competency C3: Electric Current, Potential Difference & Ohm's Law

#### Task ID: `SCI-10-ELEC-3.1`
- **Title:** Verify Ohm's Law experimentally by plotting V-I characteristics for a nichrome resistance wire.
- **Time Estimate:** 40 min
- **Resource Level:** High / Medium (battery eliminator/cells, key, ammeter, voltmeter, rheostat, nichrome wire, connecting wires)
- **Skill Type:** Circuit Assembly & Quantitative Graphing
- **Assessment Mode:** Performance Task & V-I Graph Sheet
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Connects ammeter in series and voltmeter in parallel across test nichrome wire.
  - Varies circuit current using rheostat/cells; records $V$ and $I$ across 5 trials.
  - Plots $V$ on Y-axis vs $I$ on X-axis; verifies straight line passing through origin ($V \propto I$).
  - Calculates wire resistance $R$ from graph slope: $R = \frac{\Delta V}{\Delta I}$.
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 10 Science, Chapter 12, pp. 200–204; CBSE Science Practical Skills.
- **Scaffold Support:** Circuit schematic layout card with labeled terminals and polarities ($+/-$).
- **Extension Challenge:** "How does the resistance of a metallic conductor change when temperature rises? Explain why the V-I curve bends for a hot bulb filament."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: ओम का नियम (Ohm ka Niyam), विभवांतर (Vibhavaantar - Potential Difference), प्रतिरोध (Pratirodh - Resistance)
  - Gujarati: ઓહ્મનો નિયમ (Ohmno Niyam)

### Sub-Competency C4: Series-Parallel Resistance & Joule's Heating Effect

#### Task ID: `SCI-10-ELEC-4.1`
- **Title:** Calculate equivalent resistance and branch currents in a mixed series-parallel domestic circuit.
- **Time Estimate:** 40 min
- **Resource Level:** Low (circuit diagram worksheet, board exam calculation ledger)
- **Skill Type:** Circuit Analysis & Multi-Step Physics Problem Solving
- **Assessment Mode:** Written Examination Item & Self-Check
- **Track Alignment:** Track A
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Simplifies parallel branches using $\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} \dots$.
  - Adds series elements to calculate total circuit equivalent resistance $R_{eq}$.
  - Computes total current drawn from supply using $I = \frac{V}{R_{eq}}$.
  - Explains 3 key reasons why domestic house wiring uses parallel circuits rather than series circuits (independent switches, full 220V voltage to each appliance, fault in one appliance does not break whole circuit).
- **Source Citation:** NCERT Grade 10 Science, Chapter 12, pp. 210–216; CBSE Board Exam Exemplar.
- **Scaffold Support:** Equivalent circuit step-by-step reduction diagram.
- **Extension Challenge:** "An electric iron draws 4A at 220V for 2 hours daily. Calculate monthly electrical bill at ₹6.50 per commercial kilowatt-hour (kWh) unit."
- **Multilingual Terms:**
  - Hindi: श्रेणीक्रम (Shreneekram - Series), पार्श्वक्रम (Paarshvakram - Parallel), जूल का तापन नियम (Joule ka Taapan Niyam)
  - Kannada: ಸರಣಿ ಮತ್ತು ಸಮಾಂತರ ಜೋಡಣೆ (Sarani matthu Samaantara Jodane)
