# Multidisciplinary Project Blueprint (Grade 6)
## "Community Heritage & Water Resilience: Stepwells, Rainwater Mapping & Local Governance"

**Target Grade Band:** Middle Stage (Grade 6, Ages 11–12 - Foundational Bridge)  
**Curricular Integration:** Science + Mathematics + AI (Data Basics) + Social Science (Panchayati Raj & Harappan Heritage)  
**Duration:** 3 Weeks (Integrated across 10–12 class periods)  
**Real-World Context:** Indian villages, towns, and cities face acute summer water stress despite rich historical traditions of indigenous rainwater harvesting (Baolis, Kunds, Johads, Tankas, Ahar-Pynes). This project bridges ancient wisdom with modern civic governance and scientific inquiry.

---

## 1. Project Challenge Statement

> *"How can we, as junior civic scientists and historical cartographers, measure and analyze our school and community water sources, compare them with ancient Harappan and medieval stepwell engineering, and present a data-backed 'Water Resilience & Rainwater Harvesting Charter' to our local Gram Panchayat or Ward Councillor?"*

## 3. Project Phase Breakdown & Workflow

```
[ Phase 1: Historical Inquiry & Physical Audit ] (Week 1)
  ├── Examine ancient Harappan drainage and medieval stepwells (SOC-6-HARAPPA-3.1)
  ├── Measure school surfaces and compute rooftop catchment areas (MATH-6-RATIO-1.1)
  └── Model the water cycle in a sealed bottle terrarium (SCI-6-WATER-1.1)

[ Phase 2: Data Collection & Technical Design ] (Week 2)
  ├── Conduct school water audit and tally consumption points (AI-6-DATA-2.1)
  ├── Audit equity of water access across local settlement clusters (AI-6-DATA-4.1)
  └── Draw 1:100 scale rooftop rainwater harvesting schematic (SCI-6-WATER-1.3)

[ Phase 3: Civic Presentation & Charter Adoption ] (Week 3)
  ├── Roleplay Gram Sabha meeting to debate budget allocation (SOC-6-GOV-1.1)
  ├── Calculate tank storage capacity and community water security days (MATH-6-POLY-4.2)
  └── Deliver final "Water Resilience Charter" to School Principal & Ward Office
```

---

## 4. Final Cumulative Deliverables

1. **Physical 3D or Relief Model:**
   - A desktop diorama made from clay, cardboard, and recycled bottles displaying the school rooftop rainwater harvesting setup, filter layers (gravel, coarse sand, charcoal), and underground storage tank.
2. **Community Water Data Sheet:**
   - A structured spreadsheet containing 15 surveyed local water distribution points, tagged with water potability, flow rate, and maintenance status.
3. **Civic Water Charter & Petition:**
   - A formal 2-page resolution addressed to the Gram Panchayat / Municipal Ward Councillor requesting routine water testing, repair of public taps, and adoption of rainwater harvesting in public buildings.
