# Multidisciplinary Project Blueprint (Grade 10)
## "Sustainable Urban Mobility & Green Energy Masterplan: Clean Transportation, Circuit Design & Resource Planning"

**Target Grade Band:** Secondary Stage (Grade 10, Ages 15–16 - Board Examination Readiness)  
**Curricular Integration:** Science (Electricity & Chemical Reactions) + Mathematics (Quadratics, Similarity & Probability) + AI (End-to-End Project Cycle & Confusion Matrix) + Social Science (Globalization, Urban Geography & Resource Planning)  
**Duration:** 4 Weeks (Integrated across 16 class periods)  
**Real-World Context:** Rapidly expanding Indian urban agglomerations suffer severe air pollution (PM2.5 / PM10), grid congestion, traffic fatalities, and extreme heat island effects. Developing a sustainable smart mobility corridor requires rigorous circuit design, mathematical spatial modeling, machine learning congestion monitoring, and civic planning under constitutional mandates.

---

## 1. Project Challenge Statement

> *"How can we, as student urban systems engineers and civic policy planners, design a high-efficiency 5-kilometer 'Zero-Emission Urban Mobility & Solar Microgrid Masterplan' using electric circuit power optimization, quadratic braking and kinematic equations, geometric similarity scaling, an end-to-end AI vehicle classification model evaluated via confusion matrices, and sustainable land-use resource planning?"*

## 3. Project Phase Breakdown & Workflow

```
[ Phase 1: Problem Scoping & Field Geography Audit ] (Week 1)
  ├── Scope urban corridor bottleneck using 4Ws Canvas (AI-10-SCOPE-1.1)
  ├── Audit local land degradation and asphalt heat zones (SOC-10-GEOG-4.1)
  └── Draw 1:500 scale geometric blueprint of 5 km corridor (MATH-10-TRI-3.1)

[ Phase 2: Electrical Engineering & Energy Modeling ] (Week 2)
  ├── Wire parallel circuits with circuit breaker fuses (SCI-10-ELEC-4.1)
  ├── Calculate conduit electrical resistance and voltage drops (SCI-10-ELEC-3.1)
  └── Balance chemical redox reactions for clean fuel storage (SCI-10-CHEM-1.1)

[ Phase 3: AI Development & Kinematic Safety Calculations ] (Week 3)
  ├── Calculate quadratic stopping distances for electric buses (MATH-10-QUAD-2.1)
  ├── Train vehicle classifier and compute Precision, Recall, F1 (AI-10-EVAL-4.1)
  └── Calculate charging bay queue overflow probabilities (MATH-10-PROB-4.1)

[ Phase 4: Policy Synthesis & Masterplan Exhibition ] (Week 4)
  ├── Analyze global EV component trade and domestic manufacturing (SOC-10-ECON-3.1)
  └── Present comprehensive "Clean Mobility Masterplan" to Municipal Corporation
```

## 4. Final Cumulative Deliverables

1. **Multimodal Corridor Architectural Blueprint (1:500 Scale):**
   - Precise geometric layout detailing dedicated EV bus rapid transit (BRT) lanes, protected bidirectional cycle tracks, permeable paving bioswales, and rooftop solar canopies.
2. **Electrical Microgrid & Sensor Schematic Dossier:**
   - Complete technical engineering file showing parallel DC charging circuit schematics, ammeter/voltmeter monitoring points, Joule heating safety calculations ($I^2Rt$), and battery redox equations.
3. **AI Vision Confusion Matrix & Performance Report:**
   - Formal evaluation dossier demonstrating >90% Precision and >92% Recall for emergency vehicle detection at automated smart traffic intersections.
4. **Municipal Policy & Resource Stewardship White Paper:**
   - A 6-page comprehensive policy proposal submitted to the Municipal Smart City Development Corporation citing urban masterplans, SDG 11 benchmarks, and equitable green job creation.

| **Geographic & Civic Policy** | Lists generic environmental problems without zoning solutions. | Produces detailed soil/land zoning map and evaluates 1991 globalization effects on clean energy. | Formulates a transformative, economically viable municipal masterplan grounded in constitutional and environmental law. |
