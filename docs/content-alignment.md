# CBSE, NCERT & NCF Alignment Methodology

This document outlines the rigorous methodology used to align the Subjects2Skills framework with India's national curriculum standards for the 2025–26 academic cycle.

## 1. Primary Reference Frameworks

Our alignment process is anchored by three official documents:
- **NCF 2023 (National Curriculum Framework for School Education)**: We use the NCF to define the broader pedagogical approach (e.g., experiential learning, competency-based progression) and the intended learning areas for each stage (Foundational, Preparatory, Middle, Secondary).
- **CBSE Curriculum (2025-26)**: The subject-wise learning outcomes and prescribed syllabus topics dictate the priority skills and essential knowledge blocks for each grade.
- **NCERT Textbooks (2025-26)**: The official chapter structures and content progressions provide the baseline for our activity sequences and resource mapping.

## 2. Alignment Execution Process

For every grade profile, unit, and assessment task, our curriculum team follows a three-step translation process:

### Step 1: Mapping the Core (Grade Level)
- **Review**: We analyze the CBSE syllabus for a specific grade.
- **Translate**: Broad syllabus statements are translated into observable *Priority Skills*.
- **Document**: We update the `GradeProfile` to explicitly list the CBSE core subjects and direct links to the relevant NCERT textbooks for that grade.

### Step 2: Designing the Application (Units & Lessons)
- **Reference**: When creating a sample unit (e.g., Grade 6 Science), we align it directly to a specific NCERT chapter (e.g., "Separation of Substances").
- **Embed**: We embed the exact CBSE competency codes (where available) and NCF pedagogical mandates into the unit metadata.
- **Action**: Lessons are designed to move students from theoretical understanding (textbook reading) to practical application (skill demonstration).

### Step 3: Measuring the Outcome (Assessments & Rubrics)
- **Assess**: Assessment tasks are constructed to generate verifiable evidence of the targeted CBSE competencies.
- **Evaluate**: Graduated rubrics (Emerging → Transfer) are designed to provide formative feedback on these specific skills, aligning with the NCF's mandate for continuous, holistic assessment.

## 3. Resource Curation Strategy

We prioritize a curated hub of free, open-source, and highly verifiable tools.
- **Selection Criteria**: Resources must be free (or open-source), mapped to the Indian context (e.g., DIKSHA, local Khan Academy), and practically useful in low-resource environments where possible.
- **Verification**: Every resource URL and license is checked to ensure it remains active and appropriate for K-12 use.

## 4. Maintenance & Review Cadence

Curriculum standards evolve. To maintain trust and authority:
- **Annual Audit**: A full review of all grade profiles, NCERT links, and mapped competencies is conducted annually (typically in March/April, preceding the new academic year).
- **Continuous Feedback**: We rely on teacher feedback to identify broken links or suggest new open-source tools.
- **Timestamping**: All major curriculum objects (Grades, Units, Assessments, Resources) carry a `lastUpdated` or `lastVerified` timestamp (e.g., "2025-09") to provide transparency to educators.
