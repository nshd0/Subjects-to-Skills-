## 1. General Studies & Critical Inquiry (Secondary Stage)

### 1.1 Scope & Philosophy
General Studies in the Secondary Stage (Grades 9–12) serves as the **interdisciplinary connective tissue** bridging specialized academic disciplines with real-world citizenship. It aims to develop active democratic discernment, media literacy, constitutional awareness, and ethical decision-making.

### 1.2 Core Competencies
1. **C-GS-01 (Media Literacy & Information Discernment):** Analyzes news broadcasts and social media algorithms; identifies bias, sensationalism, deepfakes, and misinformation using cross-referencing and verification tools (fact-checking portals, metadata reverse-image searches).
2. **C-GS-02 (Constitutional Ethics & Fundamental Rights):** Evaluates landmark Supreme Court judgements on privacy, equality, and free speech; defends constitutional secularism and social inclusion in simulated parliamentary committees.
3. **C-GS-03 (Contemporary Geopolitics & Climate Justice):** Examines global climate negotiation frameworks (COP summits, Paris Agreement) from the perspective of developing nations and carbon budgets.
4. **C-GS-04 (Interdisciplinary Science & Society):** Weighs ethical consequences of emerging technologies (CRISPR gene editing, generative AI copyright, autonomous surveillance drones).

### 1.3 Pedagogical Approaches
- **The Structured Academic Controversy (SAC):** Instead of winner-take-all debates, student pairs research opposing sides of a civic dilemma (*e.g., "Should private digital communication have backdoors for national security?"*), present each other's views accurately, and synthesize a consensus policy memo.
- **Investigative Civic Audits:** Teams investigate a municipal issue in their neighborhood (e.g., solid waste segregation compliance, public bus frequency during peak hours) and submit a 3-page policy brief to local ward counselors.

## 3. Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) Stand-Alone Curriculum Guides

### 3.1 Mathematics & Astronomy (Ganita & Jyotisha)
- **Baudhayana Sulba Sutras (800 BCE):** Geometric construction of ritual fire altars (*Yajna Kundas*); early formulation of the Pythagorean theorem:
  $$\text{The diagonal of a rectangle produces by itself both the areas which the length and breadth produce separately.}$$
- **Aryabhata (Aryabhatiya, 499 CE):**
  - Calculation of $\pi \approx 3.1416$.
  - Heliocentric implications: recognition that the apparent daily motion of stars is caused by the axial rotation of the Earth ($Bhu-bhramana$).
  - Formulation of table of half-chords (*Jya*), laying the foundation of modern Trigonometry.
- **Brahmagupta (Brahmasphutasiddhanta, 628 CE):**
  - First formal mathematical treatise defining Zero as a number with distinct algebraic properties ($a + 0 = a$, $a \times 0 = 0$).
  - Rules for negative numbers (*Rina* / debt) and positive numbers (*Dhana* / fortune).
- **Madhava of Sangamagrama & Kerala School of Astronomy (14th Century):**
  - Discovery of infinite series expansions for $\sin(x)$, $\cos(x)$, and $\arctan(x)$ (the Madhava-Leibniz series) over two centuries prior to European calculus.
  - Practical class activity: Reconstructing the Madhava $\pi$ series using simple polygon approximation strips.

### 3.2 Traditional Ecological Knowledge & Sustainable Architecture (Vrikshayurveda & Vastu)
- **Indigenous Water Engineering:**
  - *Stepwells (Baolis / Vavs):* Architectural masterpieces of Gujarat and Rajasthan combining underground cooling, aquifer replenishment, and social community gathering spaces.
  - *Johads and Apatani Wet-Rice Fish Systems:* Zero-waste indigenous earthen check dams in Alwar, Rajasthan (rejuvenated by Tarun Bharat Sangh); multi-tiered bamboo canal irrigation in Arunachal Pradesh preserving fertile mountain runoff.
- **Sacred Groves (Devrais / Kavu):**
  - Traditional community-protected virgin forest fragments serving as inviolable biodiversity reservoirs, seed banks, and medicinal plant sanctuaries across the Western Ghats and Meghalaya.
- **Ayurvedic Seasonal Living (Ritucharya):**
  - The science of aligning dietary consumption with local seasonal biomes (*Shishira, Vasanta, Grishma, Varsha, Sharad, Hemanta*), promoting zero-mile seasonal eating and immunity balance.

### 3.3 Classical Philosophical Traditions & Ethical Reasoning
- **Nyaya Epistemology (Pramana Vada):**
  - The four valid sources of knowledge: *Pratyaksha* (direct sensory perception), *Anumana* (inference), *Upamana* (analogy), and *Shabda* (reliable testimony/evidence).
  - Classroom Application: Teaching students scientific verification by testing claims against the 4 Pramanas.
- **Vaisheshika Atomic Theory (Kanada):**
  - Early philosophical atomic conceptualization (*Paramanu*) as the indivisible, indestructible fundamental constituent of matter.
- **Literature, Ethics & Storytelling:**
  - *Panchatantra & Hitopadesha:* Animal fables designed for political and interpersonal statecraft, demonstrating diplomacy, alliance formation (*Mitra-bheda*), and strategic prudence.
  - *Tamil Sangam Literature (Thinai Landscape Poetry):* Direct emotional and ecological coupling of human emotion with 5 geographical ecotones (Kurinji/Mountains, Mullai/Forests, Marutham/Agriculture, Neithal/Coast, Palai/Desert).
