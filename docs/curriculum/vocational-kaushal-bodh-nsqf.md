## 1. Vocational Philosophy: Dignity of Labor & Hand-Mind Integration

NEP 2020 mandates that every student in Indian schools acquire hands-on exposure to vocational crafts between Grades 6 and 8 through a dedicated **10-day Bagless Period (Kaushal Bodh)**. In Grades 9 and 10, this exposure transitions into formal credit-bearing **NSQF Level 1 & 2 Skill Electives**, preparing students for industrial apprenticeships, entrepreneurial self-reliance, and advanced technical polytechnics.

## 2. Middle Stage (Grades 6–8): 10-Day Bagless Day Craft Modules

### 2.1 Overview of 5 Local Trades

| Trade Module | Tool Kit & Materials | Core Learning Outcome | Safety Precautions | Community Master Artisan Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trade A: Basic Carpentry & Woodcraft** | Hand saw, sandpaper, C-clamps, wooden mallets, non-toxic wood polish, scrap timber blocks. | Measures, cuts, sands, and joins two wood pieces using a halved joint to build a mobile stand or pencil box. | Safety goggles mandatory; clamps used before sawing; dust masks during sanding. | Local village carpenter or carpenter cooperative craftsman. |
| **Trade B: Pottery & Terracotta Claycraft** | Terracotta clay, manual pottery wheels, wire cutting loops, modeling wooden tools, kiln/pit fire. | Prepares clay without air pockets; centers clay on wheel; throws a uniform diya, tumbler, or seed planter pot. | Hand barrier cream; aprons; safe washing of clay slurry to avoid drain blockages. | Traditional potter (Kumbhar / Gramin Shilpkar). |
| **Trade C: Textile Craft, Tailoring & Embroidery** | Sewing needles, embroidery hoops, thread skeins, thimbles, fabric scraps, button sets. | Threads needles independently; executes 4 basic hand stitches (running, backstitch, hem, French knot); sews buttons securely. | Thimble usage on pushing finger; counting needles before and after each session. | Local tailor or self-help group (SHG) textile artisan. |
| **Trade D: Household Electrical Maintenance** | Multimeter, wire strippers, insulated screwdrivers, LED bulbs, 2-pin plugs, test lamps. | Tests continuity with multimeter; strips wires without nicking copper strands; wires a functional extension board. | Zero live circuits during practice; 9V DC battery drills only before mains demonstration. | Licensed electrician from local ITI or DISCOM. |
| **Trade E: Organic Kitchen Gardening & Composting** | Hand trowels, seed starter trays, compost bins, coco peat, neem oil spray, heirloom seeds. | Formulates potting mix (soil, sand, compost 1:1:1); starts vegetable seeds; constructs aerobic compost pit. | Rubber gloves when handling bio-manure; tetanus vaccination verification; hand hygiene. | Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) extension officer or local farmer. |

### 2.2 10-Day Structured Module Day-by-Day Progression
- **Day 1: Orientation & Artisan Interaction:** Visit the workshop or invite the master craftsperson to class. Observe trade tools and listen to the oral history and cultural pride of the craft.
- **Day 2: Material Science & Tool Identification:** Understand raw materials (types of wood, clay temper, fabric fibers, copper gauges). Learn correct names and ergonomic grips for each tool.
- **Day 3–4: Fundamental Technique Practice:** Practice micro-skills (sawing straight lines along pencil marks, throwing clay coils, stitching straight seams on canvas, stripping insulation).
- **Day 5: Mid-Module Safety & Precision Check:** Complete the safety check drill. No student advances to product assembly without clearing tool handling safety verification.
- **Day 6–8: Individual / Pair Product Construction:** Craft the final usable object (e.g., wooden spice rack, terracotta flower vase, embroidered cotton bag, wired test lamp).
- **Day 9: Quality Finishing & Packaging:** Sanding, burnishing, painting with natural pigments, or labeling electrical specs.
- **Day 10: Kaushal Mela (School Exhibition & Peer Review):** Students present their finished crafts to parents, peers, and the artisan panel, explaining the steps and cost of materials.

## 3. Secondary Stage (Grades 9–10): Formal NSQF Skill Electives

In Grades 9 and 10, students can opt for an official CBSE Skill Subject (Subject Codes 401–420) equivalent to **NSQF Level 1 (Grade 9)** and **NSQF Level 2 (Grade 10)**.

### 3.1 Mapping of 6 Key Skill Sectors

| Sector | CBSE Code | NSQF Level | Core Competency Modules | Practical / Internship Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Information Technology (IT)** | 402 | Level 2 | Digital Documentation (LibreOffice Writer / MS Word), Electronic Spreadsheet (Calc / Excel), Database Management (Base / Access). | Create a 10-page formatted technical report with table of contents, mail merge, and automated macro. |
| **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** | 417 | Level 2 | Python for Data, Computer Vision (OpenCV), Natural Language Processing (NLTK), AI Project Cycle & Ethics. | Develop an AI prototype (e.g., classroom waste sorting image classifier using Teachable Machine / Python). |
| **Retail Operations** | 401 | Level 2 | Store display visual merchandising, POS billing procedures, stock inventory control, customer conflict resolution. | 15-day simulation log operating a student school co-operative store counter with POS cash registers. |
| **Agriculture & Gardening** | 408 | Level 2 | Soil health management, vermicomposting, micro-irrigation (drip/sprinkler), organic pest control (Neemastra). | Establish and manage a 100 sq ft school organic vegetable plot; submit soil test report and harvest ledger. |
| **Tourism & Hospitality** | 406 | Level 2 | Tourism geography of India, heritage itinerary design, customer communication etiquette, hotel front-office software. | Author a 5-day heritage tourism itinerary for an Indian state, including costing, hotel bookings, and eco-protocols. |
| **Mass Media Studies** | 415 | Level 2 | News script writing, digital audio recording & editing (Audacity), single-camera video shooting, photojournalism ethics. | Produce a 3-minute video news feature documenting an environmental or civic issue in the local municipal ward. |
