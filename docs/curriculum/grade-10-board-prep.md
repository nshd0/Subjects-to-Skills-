## 1. Grade 10 Overview & Assessment Architecture

Grade 10 marks the culmination of the Secondary Stage. The examination architecture aligns with the **National Assessment Centre (PARAKH)** and **CBSE 2025–26 guidelines**, mandating that at least **50% of board questions be Competency-Based** (Case Studies, Source-Based Analysis, Assertion-Reasoning, and Data Interpretation).

### Overall Assessment Split (Per Subject · 100 Marks Total)
1. **Board Theory Examination (80 Marks):**
   - 50% Competency-Based / Analytical Application items.
   - 20% Objective / Multiple-Choice items.
   - 30% Short & Long Constructive Explanations.
2. **Internal Assessment (20 Marks):**
   - Periodic Tests & Pen-Paper Assessments (5 Marks).
   - Multiple Diverse Assessment Strategies (quizzes, concept maps, oral debates) (5 Marks).
   - Student Portfolio & Self-Reflective Journal (5 Marks).
   - Subject Enrichment Activities / Mandatory Lab Practicals & Viva (5 Marks).

## 3. Mandatory Science Lab Practical Competencies & Viva Sheet

Every Grade 10 student must complete and document 6 core practical investigations in their official laboratory record notebook:

1. **Practical 1 (Physics - Ohm's Law):** Studying the dependence of potential difference ($V$) across a resistor on the current ($I$) passing through it and determining its resistance. Plotting a $V-I$ graph.
   - *Key Evaluation Criterion:* Correct circuit assembly without short circuits, accurate zero-error calibration on ammeter/voltmeter, and calculation of slope $R = \frac{\Delta V}{\Delta I}$.
2. **Practical 2 (Physics - Optics):** Finding the focal length of a concave mirror and a convex lens by obtaining the image of a distant object on a white screen.
   - *Key Evaluation Criterion:* Measurement of focal length to within $\pm 0.5\text{ cm}$ of nominal value; sharp focus verification.
3. **Practical 3 (Chemistry - Action of Water/Acids on Metals):** Performing and observing the actions of $Zn, Fe, Cu, Al$ metals on solutions of $ZnSO_4, FeSO_4, CuSO_4, Al_2(SO_4)_3$ to establish the reactivity series.
   - *Key Evaluation Criterion:* Tabulation of color changes (e.g., blue $CuSO_4$ turning pale green with iron nail) and formulation of net ionic displacement equations.
4. **Practical 4 (Chemistry - pH Determination):** Finding the pH of samples (dilute $HCl$, dilute $NaOH$, dilute ethanoic acid, lemon juice, water, sodium bicarbonate) using pH paper/universal indicator.
   - *Key Evaluation Criterion:* Matching color strips against standard pH color chart; classification into strong acid, weak acid, neutral, weak base, strong base.
5. **Practical 5 (Biology - Stomatal Preparation):** Preparing a temporary mount of a leaf peel (Tradescantia / Lily) to observe stomata under high-power compound microscope.
   - *Key Evaluation Criterion:* Epidermal peel thickness without air bubbles; correct staining with safranin; identification of kidney-shaped guard cells and stomatal pore.
6. **Practical 6 (Biology - Cellular Respiration):** Experimentally proving that carbon dioxide is released during aerobic respiration by germinating seeds using potassium hydroxide ($KOH$) absorption apparatus.
   - *Key Evaluation Criterion:* Airtight conical flask setup; observation of water level rise in bent delivery tube due to partial vacuum.

## 5. Board Exam Competency-Based Question Models & Strategy

### 5.1 Case-Based Question Example (Science 086 · 4 Marks)
> **Context:** An electric geyser in an apartment is rated at $2000\text{ W}, 220\text{ V}$. The household uses this geyser for an average of 3 hours every day during the winter months. The local electricity board charges ₹7.50 per unit ($kWh$).
>
> 1. Calculate the current drawn by the geyser when connected to the 220V mains supply. *(1 Mark)*
> 2. Determine the electrical resistance of the heating element inside the geyser. *(1 Mark)*
> 3. Calculate the total energy consumed by the geyser in a 30-day month in units ($kWh$). *(1 Mark)*
> 4. What will be the monthly electricity expenditure for operating this geyser? What rating fuse ($5\text{A}, 10\text{A},$ or $15\text{A}$) must be installed in its dedicated circuit for safety? Explain why. *(1 Mark)*

### 5.2 Board Time-Management & Answer Writing Guidelines
- **The 15-Minute Reading Period Strategy:** 
  - Do NOT solve numericals on the question paper.
  - Read Section E (Case-Based Questions) first; highlight the core scientific conditions.
  - Choose between internal choice options in Section C & D immediately.
- **Answer-Structuring Precision:**
  - In 3-mark and 5-mark answers, write in numbered bullet points, not unbroken paragraphs.
  - Underline key scientific terms (*e.g., "concave mirror", "synaptic cleft", "redox"*) with pencil.
  - Reserve 15 minutes at the end of the 3-hour examination exclusively to verify coordinate signs ($+$ vs $-$) and metric unit labels ($cm, m, \Omega, W, kWh$).
