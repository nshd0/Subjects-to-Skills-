## 1. Stage Overview & Pedagogical Architecture

The Foundational Stage spans **Ages 3 to 8**, integrating early childhood care and education (ECCE) with early primary schooling (Pre-school / Balvatika 1–3, Grade 1, and Grade 2). 

Pedagogically, this stage is governed by the **Panchakosha (Five Sheaths)** holistic human development model and the **NCF-FS 2022 principles**:
1. **Sharirik Vikas (Physical Development)**: Gross and fine motor dexterity, sensory perception, and bodily hygiene.
2. **Pranik Vikas (Vital Energy & Wellbeing)**: Breath control, stamina, balanced emotional tone, and healthy routines.
3. **Manasik Vikas (Mental & Emotional Development)**: Sensory integration, concentration, empathy, emotional naming, and memory.
4. **Bauddhik Vikas (Intellectual & Cognitive Development)**: Emergent numeracy, classification, reasoning, and curiosity.
5. **Chaetasik Vikas (Spiritual & Aesthetic Development)**: Joy, appreciation of nature, rhythm, artistic expression, and ethical habits.

## 2. Pre-school (Balvatika 1–3 · Ages 3–6)

### 2.1 Developmental Milestones
- **Gross Motor (Physical):** Climbs stairs with alternating feet, catches a large bounced ball with two hands, balances on one foot for 4–5 seconds.
- **Fine Motor (Physical):** Uses pincer grasp for crayons and safe plastic tongs; strings large beads; tears paper with controlled wrist movements.
- **Cognitive:** Matches identical objects; classifies toys by color or size (up to 4 attributes); understands basic temporal sequences (*first we wash hands, then we eat*).
- **Language & Emergent Literacy:** Listens attentively to short 5-minute illustrated stories; uses 4–6 word sentences; recognizes rhyming syllables and environmental print.
- **Socio-Emotional:** Takes turns in small-group games with adult prompting; expresses basic feelings (happy, sad, hurt) using gestures and simple words; respects physical classroom boundaries.

### 2.2 Bridge & Transition Criteria
- **Entry Indicators (from home):** Responsive to caregiver voice, can express basic physical needs (water, toilet), engages in parallel play.
- **Readiness for Grade 1:** Can follow two-step verbal instructions; identifies 10+ common objects by sound/silhouette; demonstrates print awareness (knows books open left-to-right); counts up to 10 concrete objects reliably.

## 3. Grade 1 (Ages 6–7)

### 3.1 Developmental Milestones
- **Cognitive:** Grasps conservation of small quantities; understands one-to-one correspondence up to 20; distinguishes between living and non-living objects by movement/growth.
- **Language:** Matches individual letters (Aksharas / Alphabets) to sounds; reads 3–4 letter phonetic words with blending; writes own name legibly.
- **Physical Dexterity:** Holds a standard pencil with dynamic tripod grasp; cuts along straight and curved lines with safety scissors.
- **Socio-Emotional:** Cooperates in pair activities; initiates apologies with guided reflection; manages personal school belongings independently.

### 3.2 Bridge & Transition Criteria
- **Incoming Bridge from Pre-school:** Familiarity with circle-time norms, recognition of cardinal numbers 1–9, oral communication of needs.
- **Readiness for Grade 2:** Fluent phonics decoding of simple sentences, addition/subtraction within 10 using fingers/counters, adherence to 30-minute classroom inquiry routine.

## 4. Grade 2 (Ages 7–8)

### 4.1 Developmental Milestones
- **Cognitive:** Performs mental addition and subtraction within 20; understands place value (tens and ones up to 99); demonstrates basic causal reasoning (*plants wilt when unwatered*).
- **Language & Literacy:** Reads grade-level passages (30–40 words per minute) with comprehension; writes simple 3-sentence descriptions with proper capital letters and full stops.
- **Socio-Emotional:** Resolves minor playground disagreements through dialogue; shows respect for classroom diversity; completes 20-minute independent work tasks.

## 5. Sample Unit Exemplar: Grade 1 Mathematics & Literacy Integration
**Unit Title:** The Fruit Stall: Counting, Shapes, and Words  
**Duration:** 2 Weeks (10 Lessons · 40 mins/day)  
**Driving Question:** "How do we help the fruit seller organize, count, and sell fresh fruits fairly using numbers and signs?"  
**Target Competencies:** `C-G1-M-01` (Numbers to 20), `C-G1-M-02` (Shapes), `C-G1-L2-01` (Oral English Vocabulary).

### Lesson Schedule & Scaffolded Instructions
1. **Day 1–2: Sensory Fruit Discovery:** Bring guavas, bananas, apples, oranges. Group by roundness vs elongation. Practice oral adjectives (*sweet, rough, yellow*).
2. **Day 3–5: Bundling Tens and Ones:** Use clay-molded fruit models. Make baskets of 10 bananas each. 1 basket + 4 single bananas = 14 bananas.
3. **Day 6–8: Price Tag Writing & Role-Play:** Write numerals on leaf cards (₹2, ₹5, ₹10). Students role-play buyer and seller using simulated tokens.
4. **Day 9: Formative Exit Ticket:** Distribute printable visual exit ticket below.
5. **Day 10: Celebration Fruit Chaat Party:** Students prepare a healthy fruit plate, counting spoonfuls.
