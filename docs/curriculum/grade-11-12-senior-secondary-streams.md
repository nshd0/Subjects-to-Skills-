## 1. Senior Secondary Stage Architecture & NEP 2020 Flexibility

Under **NEP 2020** and **NCF-SE 2023**, the Senior Secondary Stage (Grades 11–12 · Ages 16–18) eliminates rigid silos between academic streams, vocational trades, and extracurricular pursuits. While students select a major disciplinary focus, they can cross-enroll across 4 major stream specializations:

1. **Science & Engineering Stream (STEM):** Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science, Biotechnology.
2. **Commerce & Financial Management Stream:** Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Applied Mathematics, Financial Markets.
3. **Humanities & Social Sciences Stream:** Political Science, History, Sociology, Psychology, Geography, Legal Studies, Philosophy.
4. **Applied Skills & Emerging Technologies Stream:** Artificial Intelligence (Skill Subject 843), Data Science, Design Thinking, Agriculture, Health Care, Mass Media Studies.

## 2. Stream Overviews, Career Pathways & Subject Combinations

### 2.1 Science Stream (STEM & Medical)
- **Core Subject Packages:**
  - *Engineering Track (PCM):* Physics (042) + Chemistry (043) + Mathematics (041) + Computer Science (083) or English Core (301).
  - *Medical / Life Sciences Track (PCB):* Physics (042) + Chemistry (043) + Biology (044) + Psychology (037) / Biotechnology (045).
  - *Interdisciplinary Dual-Science Track (PCMB):* Physics + Chemistry + Mathematics + Biology.
- **Higher Education & Career Trajectories:**
  - B.Tech / B.E. (IIT-JEE, State CETs) in Artificial Intelligence, Aerospace, Mechanical, Electrical, Biochemical Engineering.
  - MBBS / BDS / AYUSH / Allied Health Sciences (NEET-UG).
  - Pure Scientific Research (IISER, NISER, IISC - BS-MS Research programmes).
  - Bioinformatics, Quantitative Finance, Data Architecture.

### 2.2 Commerce & Business Stream
- **Core Subject Packages:**
  - *Professional Accounting Track:* Accountancy (055) + Business Studies (054) + Economics (030) + Mathematics (041) or Applied Mathematics (241).
  - *Entrepreneurship & Corporate Track:* Accountancy + Business Studies + Economics + Entrepreneurship (066) + Legal Studies (074).
- **Higher Education & Career Trajectories:**
  - Chartered Accountancy (ICAI CA Foundation $\to$ Intermediate $\to$ Final).
  - Company Secretary (ICSI CS), Cost & Management Accountant (ICMAI CMA).
  - B.Com (Hons), BBA, BMS, IPMAT (IIM 5-Year Integrated Management).
  - Investment Banking, Actuarial Sciences, FinTech Regulatory Compliance.

### 2.3 Humanities & Social Sciences Stream
- **Core Subject Packages:**
  - *Public Policy & Jurisprudence Track:* Political Science (028) + History (027) + Legal Studies (074) + Economics (030).
  - *Behavioral Sciences & Culture Track:* Psychology (037) + Sociology (039) + Geography (029) + English Elective (001).
- **Higher Education & Career Trajectories:**
  - Law (5-Year Integrated BA LLB / BBA LLB via CLAT & AILET).
  - Civil Services Examination (UPSC CSE), State Public Service Commissions.
  - Journalism, Digital Media, Geoinformatics, International Diplomacy (IFS).
  - Clinical Psychology, Cognitive Sciences, Heritage Management & Museology.

### 2.4 Applied Skills & Emerging Technologies Stream
- **Core Subject Packages:**
  - Artificial Intelligence (843) + Data Science (844) + Applied Mathematics (241) + Information Technology (802) + Design Thinking & Innovation.
- **Higher Education & Career Trajectories:**
  - B.Sc. in Applied AI, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security.
  - Industrial Product Design (NID, UCEED), UX/UI Design, Spatial Computing.
  - Direct Entry Industry Apprenticeships (NSQF Level 4–5), Junior Automation Specialists.

## 4. Senior Secondary Capstone & Research Project Guidelines

All Grade 11 and 12 students complete an **Interdisciplinary Capstone Project (1 Year Duration · 20 Marks)**.

### 4.1 Four-Stage Capstone Lifecycle
1. **Phase 1: Research Question Formulation (Month 1–2):** Student formulates a testable empirical question or actionable business/social problem statement. (Must cite 5+ peer-reviewed or authoritative baseline sources).
2. **Phase 2: Methodology & Data Collection (Month 3–5):**
   - *Science:* Controlled lab experiment or computer simulation.
   - *Commerce:* Financial audit of local MSME or market questionnaire of 100+ consumers.
   - *Humanities:* Qualitative field interviews, archival document analysis, or case law synthesis.
3. **Phase 3: Data Analysis & Synthesis (Month 6–8):** Statistical analysis, error margin quantification, and synthesis of findings into an academic 15-to-20 page typed report.
4. **Phase 4: Capstone Defense & Viva Voce (Month 9–10):** 10-minute presentation before a 3-member panel of teachers and an external subject expert.

## 5. Board Examination Readiness: Sample Competency Questions (Grade 12)

### 5.1 Physics (042) Case-Based Question
> **Context:** An AC generator produces an alternating EMF by rotating a coil of $N$ turns and area $A$ with constant angular frequency $\omega$ in a uniform magnetic field $B$. The instantaneous EMF is given by $e = e_0 \sin(\omega t)$, where $e_0 = NAB\omega$.
> 1. What is the phase difference between the magnetic flux linked with the coil and the induced EMF? *(1 Mark)*
> 2. If the rotation speed of the generator coil is doubled, what will happen to the maximum EMF ($e_0$) and the frequency of the AC voltage produced? *(1 Mark)*
> 3. A coil of 200 turns of cross-sectional area $0.1\text{ m}^2$ rotates at $50\text{ rev/s}$ in a magnetic field of $0.05\text{ T}$. Calculate the peak value of EMF induced in the coil. *(2 Marks)*

### 5.2 Accountancy (055) Competency Analysis Item
> **Context:** Green Agro Ltd. issued 50,000 equity shares of ₹10 each at a premium of ₹2 per share, payable ₹3 on application, ₹5 on allotment (including premium), and the balance ₹4 on first and final call. Applications were received for 70,000 shares. Allotment was made pro-rata to applicants for 60,000 shares, the remaining applications being rejected.
> 1. Calculate the excess application money adjusted towards allotment. *(1 Mark)*
> 2. Pass journal entries for receipt of application money and allotment adjustment. *(2 Marks)*
> 3. If a shareholder holding 600 shares failed to pay the allotment money, calculate the net allotment arrears amount. *(1 Mark)*

### 5.3 Political Science (028) Analytical Item
> *"The end of the Cold War did not mean the end of security dilemmas; rather, it shifted the locus of security from interstate ideological warfare to non-traditional threats."*
> - Critically evaluate this statement with reference to two non-traditional security threats: global climate change and transnational cyber espionage. *(4 Marks)*
