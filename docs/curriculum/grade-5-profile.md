## 1. Grade Profile & Developmental Milestones

### 1.1 Stage Classification & Age Span
- **Stage:** Preparatory Stage Capstone (Grade 5)
- **Target Age:** 10–11 years
- **Pedagogical Philosophy:** Consolidation of foundational and preparatory skills. Preparation for disciplinary separation in Middle Stage (Grade 6). Students transition from intuitive observations to early formal models in mathematics (decimals, volume, angles, factors), biological processes (digestion, respiration, germination), and historical-geographical contexts.

### 1.2 Key Developmental Milestones
1. **Cognitive (Mathematical Modeling):** Understands angles (acute, right, obtuse) and uses a protractor; manipulates common fractions and decimal notations (tenths, hundredths); calculates volume of rectangular prisms ($V = l \times b \times h$); identifies prime/composite numbers and factors.
2. **Cognitive (Scientific & Environmental Systems):** Explains interdependence in ecosystems (predator-prey balance, decomposers); traces energy conversion from sunlight to human food; explains food preservation techniques (pickling, drying, refrigeration).
3. **Cognitive (Social Inquiry & Spatial Geography):** Reads conventional symbols on topographical and political maps; locates major Indian river systems, mountain ranges, and state capitals; understands basic principles of the Indian Constitution (equality, justice, fraternity).
4. **Language & Critical Literacy:** Formulates comparative arguments; summarizes 250-word expository articles; distinguishes between verifiable facts and subjective opinions in print media.
5. **Socio-Emotional & Leadership:** Mediates peer discussions in group projects; demonstrates accountability for shared classroom resources; exhibits awareness of accessibility needs for differently-abled peers.

### 1.3 Bridge & Transition Criteria
- **Bridge from Grade 4 (Entry Competencies):**
  - Fluent 4-digit arithmetic and perimeter calculation.
  - Clear understanding of basic food chains, water filtration, and community civic services.
  - Writing cohesive 5–8 sentence descriptive narratives with proper punctuation.
- **Readiness Indicators for Middle Stage (Grade 6 Exit Competencies):**
  - Mathematical mastery of decimal arithmetic, percentage concepts, and area calculation.
  - Preparedness for separate disciplinary study: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Geography, Civics.
  - Independent experimental record-keeping using structured lab journals.

## 4. Sample Unit Exemplar: Grade 5 Integrated Science & Mathematics
**Unit Title:** The Architecture of Energy: Food Chains, Digestion, and Nutritional Budgets  
**Duration:** 3 Weeks (15 Sessions · 45 mins/session)  
**Driving Question:** "How does the sun's energy travel from agricultural soil to your body's muscles to power an athlete in a 100m race?"  
**Integrated Competencies:** `C-G5-EVS-01` (Digestion & Nutrition), `C-G5-M-02` (Decimals & Quantities), `C-G5-L1-01` (Expository Writing).

### 4.1 Lesson Sequence
- **Session 1–3: The Solar Engine:** Trace solar energy capture by green leaves (photosynthesis baseline). Construct food chains linking paddy $\to$ grasshopper $\to$ frog $\to$ snake $\to$ eagle.
- **Session 4–7: The Great Starch Mystery:** Place a piece of plain roti in mouth and chew 30 times without swallowing. Observe the transition from bland taste to sweet taste (salivary amylase breakdown). Diagram the alimentary canal.
- **Session 8–11: Decimal Nutritional Analysis:** Examine packaged food labels (lentils, milk, biscuits). Read protein, carb, and fat values in grams with decimal points ($6.5\text{ g}$ protein per $100\text{ mL}$).
- **Session 12–14: Design an Athlete's Balanced Diet:** Calculate the total nutritional intake for a young runner using a decimal addition budget sheet.
- **Session 15: Unit Exit Ticket & Lab Viva:** Print-ready template below.
