# Internal Post-Release Notes: V0.5.0

**Release Tag:** `v0.5.0`  
**Target Release Date:** September 22, 2026  
**Engineering Lead / Release Engineer:** Subjects2Skills Core Team  

---

## 1. What's in V0.5
- **CBSE AI & Computational Thinking Tri-Track System Fully Integrated**:
  - **Track A (Compulsory Embedded)**: Covers Grades 3–5 (50 hrs/yr Computational Thinking embedded in Math/EVS) and Grades 6–8 (100 hrs/yr CT&AI embedded in Math, Science, and Social Science).
  - **Track B (Optional Skill Module 901)**: Grades 6–8 standalone elective modules (Code 901A/B/C, 15 hrs).
  - **Track C (Elective Skill Subject 417)**: Secondary board examination subject (Grades 9–10, 100 marks: 50 theory + 50 practical, 200 hrs) and preliminary competency scaffolding for Grades 11–12.
- **Visual Distinction on Skill Nodes**: Blue (Track A), Amber (Track B), and Emerald (Track C) badges with annual hour allocations.
- **Node Detail Drawer Enhancements**: Integrated Track Profile drawer showing duration breakdown, policy transitions (NEP 2020 / NCF-SE 2023 2027–28 compulsory rollout expectations), and direct CBSE handbook citations.
- **Grade- & Track-Aware Planning Engine**:
  - Lesson Plan Wizard dynamic filtering by grade and AI track.
  - Assessment Wizard with track-aligned rubrics and Bloom's cognitive taxonomy integration.
- **Grade Hubs Upgrades**: Dedicated AI & CT curriculum sections in `Grade8Hub` and `GenericGradeHub` for all grade bands.
- **Type Safety & Build Cleanliness**: 100% clean `tsc --noEmit` validation and successful Vite production compilation.

---

## 2. What's Explicitly *Not* in V0.5 (Deferred to Subsequent Milestones)
- **Live Collaborative Multi-Teacher Editing**: Simultaneous multi-user websocket or real-time presence in lesson plan creation remains planned for future versions; current planning engine operates with local state and authenticated draft persistence.
- **Grades 11–12 Unit-Level Lesson Deep Dives**: Grades 11–12 include high-level competency maps and transition indicators, but unit-level laboratory exercise blueprints await CBSE's formal 2026-27 senior secondary subject syllabi publication.
- **Automated AI-Assisted Lesson Plan Drafting**: Direct LLM generation of lesson content is intentionally constrained to teacher-directed prompt actions, avoiding unverified content synthesis in alignment with academic trust guidelines.
- **State Board (Non-CBSE) Localization**: Curricular alignments are strictly focused on CBSE 2025–26 / 2026–27 and NCF-SE 2023. Regional state syllabus cross-walks remain out of scope for this cycle.
