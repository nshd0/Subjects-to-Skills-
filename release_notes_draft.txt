Subject: Welcome to Subjects2Skills v0.4 – Grade 8, Collaboration, and Clarity!

Hello Educators,

We're thrilled to announce the rollout of Subjects2Skills v0.4! This update brings three major new capabilities to help you map, plan, and assess with even more clarity:

1. **Grade 8 is Fully Mapped!** 🎓
You can now explore complete skill progressions across Mathematics, Science, English, Social Science, and Second Languages for Grade 8. Check out the new Grade 8 Hub to see subject readiness badges and instantly launch into planning.

2. **Cross-Subject Collaboration** 🤝
Planning an integrated unit with your colleagues just got easier. Our new Cross-Subject Collaboration tools let you invite co-teachers, select shared skills across different subjects, leave comments, and track the unit's status from draft to ready. 

3. **Visual Clarity & Onboarding** 🗺️
We've added an interactive 3D Skill Map that visualizes how competencies build over time. Plus, if you're new to the platform, a guided onboarding tour and clear "How It Works" examples (using real curriculum data!) will get you up to speed in minutes.

We can't wait for you to try these features. Log in today to start planning your next integrated unit!
